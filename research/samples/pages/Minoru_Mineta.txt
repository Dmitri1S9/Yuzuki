=== PAGE: Minoru Mineta ===
== Introduction ==
Minoru Mineta, also known as the Fresh-Picked Hero: Grape Juice, was a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School, training to become a Pro Hero.
Eight years later, Minoru has graduated from U.A. High School alongside his classmates, and previously worked as a Sidekick for Mt. Lady until splitting off to run his own agency, and becoming the No. 108 Hero.

== Appearance ==
Minoru is a short teenage boy with a large, round head and rather puffy cheeks. His nose is small, stubby, and points slightly upwards, and he has an unusually distinguishable philtrum, oval-shaped eyes with large, black pupils, and notably thin eyebrows. He has a head of short dark purple hair with four large ball-shaped clumps resembling a mohawk.
During school, he wears the standard U.A. uniform, but modified due to his short stature, with only one button on the jacket.
His hero costume is simplistic, consisting only of a purple shirt and mask with a yellow cape, boots, and gloves. His pants are white with a lighter purple trim and stick out quite a lot around his waist. Before the Joint Training Battle, he added a stem-like detail propped up on his hair, contributing to the grape theme of his costume.
Eight years after the end of the Final War, Minoru has grown considerably taller, though he is still short in comparison to his classmates. He has gained some faint stubble under his chin and has put on a bit of weight, making him rather chubby, but still fit. His hero costume has remained mostly the same, but he now adorns new arm bands and wears headphones for communications.

== Personality ==
Minoru is timid but perverted to the point of infamy, which makes him unpopular with the girls and even some of the boys, who consider his interests and mannerisms depraved and rude. His mind frequently trails off to lewd thoughts, even in completely unrelated situations. When an opportunity to peep at the girls arises, Minoru becomes determined to take it, although this often leads to painful results or him being restrained so he won't cause trouble again. Minoru's interest in women is one of his motivations to become a hero, alongside general popularity. He is unashamed of those traits, openly admitting them out loud.
To add to this, Minoru has a huge jealous streak, often envying those who are seen as cool and attract female attention, while lamenting how seemingly simple and underpowered his Quirk is compared to his classmates. Occasionally, Minoru will try to act charming and impressive, though his efforts usually fail and often make him come off as obnoxious.
Minoru can be hypocritical, criticizing others for perceived perverted behavior, even if they are just misunderstandings. Minoru is one who always speaks his mind, even when it comes off as harsh and insulting due to his comments being more on the cynical side. He also has a more teasing and goading side, even when he's not being perverted and has a habit of jeering at others for fun.
However, Minoru is not a bully and does express fear and concern when others are hurt or in danger. When putting his perverted and unpleasant character aside, there are times when he can be genuinely kind and friendly, such as when he assured Ochaco Uraraka that Izuku would recover from his surgery, or when he ran to comfort Izuku Midoriya after the latter returned to U.A. High School following the civilians' angry protest to keep him out. Furthermore, despite his crass attitude, Minoru isn't beyond realizing when he may have offended someone, as seen when he apologized to Mezo Shoji for calling him an octopus while the latter explained his past of being abused because of his appearance.
Minoru easily freaks out in moments of great stress or fear, acting impulsively, crying when the situation does not look favorable, and abusing his Quirk recklessly, to the point of hurting himself. Minoru isn't a very confident fighter and may attempt to flee in highly disadvantageous situations whenever the opportunity presents itself. During his time at U.A. however, Minoru has gradually become braver as he's continued to learn and grow alongside his classmates, standing his ground even when overpowered and clearly terrified.
While Minoru is well known for freaking out and crying excessively, more serious events can lead him to a state of severe shock and denial, something shown just after the Paranormal Liberation War, where he was clearly traumatized upon witnessing the deaths of several Heroes, saying that the whole thing must have been a horrible nightmare. Likewise, when Yuga Aoyama was discovered to be an unwilling mole for All For One, Minoru refused to believe that his classmate could have willingly betrayed them and that he must have been brainwashed.
Minoru is shown to be surprisingly intelligent, able to score very high on tests and be in the top half of Class 1-A's grades, despite being considered a slacker by most of the class. At the same time, though, he also displays a very smug and complacent side whenever his aptitude is noticed, and his confidence often proves to be quite unbalanced: either strongly doubting himself or feeling highly overconfident. Additionally, Minoru can develop creative and competent strategies to achieve victory, pushing through his flaws when needed. This intuition was best seen when he outsmarted Midnight and passed the Practical Exam.
Another one of Minoru's ambitions is to be popular, trying everything in his power to be loved by everyone, oblivious of his perverted behavior being the reason why he isn't. His attempts usually fail, as everything he does gets him nowhere in popularity, only being good friends with Denki Kaminari and Izuku Midoriya and having an on-and-off friendship with Tsuyu Asui, while the rest of the class doesn't talk to him often. While Minoru wants to be cool or popular, he is frowned upon by many girls and even some boys due to his personality.
Despite his bad qualities and nervousness, Minoru proves he can rise to the occasion when needed. He can showcase his fierce courage and aptitude when it truly matters and can usually be reinforced with encouragement. He shows this most of all during times such as the Provisional Hero Licensing Exam, as well as when he helps his classmates face incredibly powerful and dangerous opponents such as All Might (who was masquerading as a villain), Nine, and Gigantomachia. Minoru can also be just as heroic and selfless as the rest of Class 1-A. During the Final War, even after he and his companions were ruthlessly defeated by All For One, he used his remaining strength to plead to the villain to spare his classmate Fumikage Tokoyami and to take his Quirk instead, even though this would have certainly ended his dream of becoming a Hero.
Following the Final War, Minoru has graduated from U.A. High School, becoming an independent Pro Hero after taking down a major Villain. While he has grown and proven himself to be a capable Hero since enrolling and eventually graduating from U.A., his perverted tendencies are implied to still get the better of him in his budding career, as he's been heavily criticized for sending private messages to several of his (presumably female) fans. Even so, he's gained a small, covert following of those who admire the sparks of his bravery when the situation calls for it, and is making an active, arduous effort to truly improve himself.

== Abilities ==
Overall Abilities: Minoru may not be the direct-combat type, but he does have an excellent handle on his Quirk, Pop Off. Minoru primarily uses his spheres as projectiles, binding opponents and their weapons to objects or themselves. Minoru can take advantage of the fact that his spheres bounce off his body to use them as small trampolines, allowing him to bypass obstacles on the ground. He also has impressive camaraderie, using his Pop Off spheres to bind opponents, enabling his allies to defeat them successfully, and vice versa. Shortly before the Provisional Hero License Exam, Minoru came up with a makeshift bead weapon composed of his Pop Off spheres, which aids him in defending against projectile attacks. His abilities eventually garnered the attention of The Lurkers (consisting of Edgeshot, Kamui Woods, and Mt. Lady), giving Minoru the opportunity to intern under the trio of powerful heroes and improve on close and efficient team maneuvers.
Keen Intellect: Despite his lewd personality, Minoru is quite intelligent, scoring 9th place in the midterms, displaying his skill in academics, something made more impressive by the fact he is never seen studying. Minoru is also very clever in battle, such as using his Pop Off Quirk to create effective traps for which his spheres are very suitable. Pop Off tends to be a relatively underestimated ability, which allows Minoru to surprise those who let their guard down. He can also employ unorthodox strategies, even ones befitting his perverted nature. At the U.A. Sports Festival, knowing how physically conditioned Momo Yaoyorozu is, Minoru used his Pop Off balls to latch onto her back so he could pass the Obstacle Race portion of the festival.
During the Cavalry Battle, he teamed up with Mezo Shoji and Tsuyu Asui and devised an effective strategy that involved the two smaller students latching onto Mezo's back while shrouded by the latter's Dupli-Arms and, in tandem, Minoru traps his opponents with his Pop Off balls, while Tsuyu uses her tongue to take the other contestants' points. Minoru's ingenuity was also showcased when he and Hanta Sero were paired together against Midnight for the First Term Final Exam. Minoru is knowledgeable of heroes with lascivious personalities and knows of Midnight's sadistic side that arises when her opponent runs away from her. Taking advantage of this trait, Minoru pretended to flee and lured her into a trap using his "Grape Rush" Ultimate Move to bind the R-Rated Hero and secure victory for both him and Hanta.
Artistic Talent: Minoru has shown to be good at drawing. Alongside Manga Fukidashi, he served as the lead artist in a manga that featured a story about U.A.'s Hero course, with his skills only improving as he refined his character designs.

== Quirk ==
Pop Off: Minoru's Quirk allows him to produce and pluck ball-shaped objects from his head; the balls grow back as fast as they're plucked. The balls are incredibly sticky and will stick to any surface; the sole exception is Minoru, as he bounces off them (since he is also made of them). His overall health determines the duration of their adhesive quality. He can pull many of these objects from his head; however, if he plucks too many, it will cause him to start bleeding from his scalp.
As part of his training, Minoru continuously pulled off the balls on his head to increase their adhesiveness, strengthen his scalp, and reduce the risk of bleeding from his head despite suffering from the said injury during his training.

== Ultimate Moves ==
Grape Rush: Minoru rushes towards the enemy and throws many of his sticky objects at the enemy while running toward them, sticking the enemy in place and allowing Minoru to rush past them.
Super Grape Rush: From an elevated position, Minoru launches a large number of his sticky spheres to trap the enemy between objects.
Grape Buckler: Minoru attaches a number of Pop Off Spheres to a shield, which he can use for defense while simultaneously trapping whatever hits the shield. He first used this technique to block projectiles launched by Twin Impact during the fifth round of the Joint Training Battle.
Grape-Pinky Combo Mineta Bounce: After Mina Ashido uses Acid Layback to spin on the spot and Minoru lays down multiple Pop Off Spheres on the ground and walls, Mina throws Minoru with great force so that he bounces from sphere to sphere. As he is flying through the air and bouncing off of each sphere, he can throw more Pop Off Spheres from his head onto his opponents while making it difficult to be hit back. They first displayed this technique during the fifth round of the Joint Training Battle.
Vineyard: Minoru performs this move by creating a vineyard with his Pop Off Spheres which acts as a snare. He first used this technique to immobilize Team 5-B during Round 5 before being interrupted by Reiko Yanagi's Poltergeist.

== Equipment ==
Hero Costume: Minoru's hero costume was made with the purpose of letting him use his Quirk without his grapes sticking to it.
The Grape Face Mask: This equipment is a simple face mask that covers almost all of Minoru's face and leaves the top of his head exposed so that he can grab his grape-like hairs.
The Gloves that Pluck: This equipment was made using the special substance that exudes from Minoru's scalp, this was to prevent his grapes from sticking to them. However, this is only for the palms of the gloves.
The Grape Pants: This equipment was designed to have nubs that look like a pattern that were modeled after his grape-like hair and have almost the same stickiness as them. This was so that he could hold small objects on his person.
The Minoru Mantle: This equipment was originally desired to flutter in the wind. However, the designers decided to make it shorter so that it wouldn't get stuck on his hair.
In addition to his costume, he also wears what appears to be a grape stem on his head. While it has not been formally addressed, it is presumably some kind of support item.

== Trivia ==
Minoru's preliminary design had him as slightly taller and wearing a more unique pair of shoes, resembling those that Izuku Midoriya usually wears. His costume also had additional details like a stem on the upper part of his mask, further referencing his grape theme.
He would later implement this stem detail some time before the Joint Training Battle.
His overall design was inspired by a cluster of grapes.
According to Minoru's Volume 2 profile:
Minoru's favorite things are girls.
Minoru's character concept was conceived early.
Kohei Horikoshi has trouble balancing Minoru's likeability with his perversion.
Horikoshi enjoys drawing Minoru, viewing a bit of himself in him.
Horikoshi details how Minoru was able to pass the U.A. Entrance Exam, noting that binding the Villain Bots to walls or blocking their cannons with Pop Off were both viable strategies.
According to Minoru's Ultra Analysis profile:
His Mineta Beads were inspired by something the databook refuses to mention to its young audience.
His personality describes him as a "perverted straight shooter".
Minoru's surname contains the kanji for "summit" and "rice field", and his first name contains "to bear fruit, great fertility, berry". The kanji "峰 mine" is included in the word for "giant grapes", "巨峰 kyohō".
Minoru's known U.A. data is as follows:
Student No. 19 in Class 1-A.
Ranked 19th during Shota Aizawa's Quirk Apprehension Test.
Ranked 9th in Class 1-A's mid-term grades.
Ranked in the Top 42 of the U.A. Sports Festival First-Year Stage.
Minoru's rankings in the Popularity Polls are as follows:
Minoru ranked 18th in the First Popularity Poll.
Minoru ranked 31st in the Second Popularity Poll.
Minoru ranked 45th in the Third Popularity Poll.
Not counting children and anime-original characters, Minoru is the shortest human character in the main series, at 108 cm (or 3' 6½").
Minoru shares his birthday with Atsuhiro Sako.
Minoru, Momo Yaoyorozu, Mina Ashido, and Tsuyu Asui all share the same costume designer.
In the English dub of the anime, Minoru speaks with a notable lisp, which his voice actor implemented in an effort to make him sound cuter and to tone down his perverted character.

== Class 1-A ==
Most of Class 1-A has a fairly low opinion of Minoru due to his perverted antics and cowardly nature. The boys and girls alike tend to scold or punish him whenever he does or says something lewd, and this carries over to the rest of the staff. Despite this, Minoru does make a few friends during his time at U.A., and the class does generally acknowledge his power growth over time.

== Izuku Midoriya ==
Izuku is a fellow student of Minoru's in Class 1-A, sitting in front of Minoru in class. Minoru has come to admire Izuku for his acts of heroism and for believing in his abilities as a hero, although he is somewhat skeptical at first of Izuku's determination. Minoru's perverse antics usually gross Izuku out, with the most noticeable occurrence being in Save! Rescue Training, where Minoru is forbidden to carry any female rescues due to inquiring about holding their behinds. Despite this, Izuku is not as vocal about it as others are.
The two have become friends, particularly following their battle together against the League of Villains. He was one of the people who went to see Izuku after his loss to Shoto during the U.A. Sports Festival and became frantic along with the others upon hearing that he required surgery, showing that Minoru cares for his wellbeing. They have also been seen talking to each other and hanging out in-between classes on occasion.
He was quite amazed to see that Izuku overcame his drawbacks and improve in the Rescue training race.
It appears he has picked up some of Izuku's habits, as he used his own knowledge of heroes' Quirks to defeat Midnight. Minoru also engaged in commentary with Izuku during the Joint Training Battle.
Like Denki, Minoru has been shown too be slightly jealous of Izuku gaining affection from females. This was first shown when Hanta made the claim that Camie Utsushimi (who was actually Himiko Toga in disguise) was hiding with Izuku while naked, with Minoru specifically screaming and assuming he crossed "several bases" with her at that time. A tamer instance of this happened earlier during their time at I-Expo, when they, along with the other girls in attendance, thought Izuku was romantically involved with Melissa Shield in secret, with Minoru hungrily negging his friend to "stop being greedy" and introduce him and Denki to her. Later on, when Izuku and Ochaco after they share a moment in front of the whole class, Minoru deduced the budding relationship between the two and quickly became jealous.
During the battle between Class 1-A and Deku, Minoru says that he never thought the power of One For All was amazing, but what made Minoru admire Izuku was how he leads everyone to victory despite being scared himself. Upon Izuku's return to U.A., Minoru was the most desperate to comfort him when he broke down into tears, running up to him before being stopped by Tenya and then running up to him later alongside Kota to hug him. Minoru also joined the others in bathing Izuku and agreed with him on forgiving Yuga Aoyama for unwillingly being All For One's mole within U.A. High.

=== PAGE: Minoru Mineta/Synopsis ===
== History ==
When he was in middle school, Minoru Mineta felt intense envy and resentment towards the students who were very popular with girls. This desire for female admiration led him to define the reason he wanted to become a hero: He believed if he managed to become one, he would also be popular.
In order to achieve his dreams, he managed to pass the entrance exams and became a student at U.A. High School.

== Quirk Apprehension Test Arc ==
Minoru Mineta is first seen sitting in homeroom when Shota Aizawa enters the room. He watches Mezo Shoji do the grip strength test and later utilizes his Quirk during the sideways jump test. He ends up scoring second lowest, only before Izuku Midoriya. He is soon shocked to find out that Shota's threat of expulsion was just a "rational deception" so that the class would do their best.

== Battle Trial Arc ==
Minoru is paired with Momo Yaoyorozu on "Team C", much to his joy. He, along with the rest of his classmates, watch Izuku and Katsuki Bakugo fight each other. At the end of the trials, Minoru comments on how cool All Might is.
When it is their turn, the Team C embodies the villains in the Battle Trial, facing Team G, made up of Denki and Kyoka. During the test, Momo gets mad at Mineta for doing nothing but leering at her.

== U.S.J. Arc ==
At the Unforeseen Simulation Joint, Minoru watches as villains invade the building. Minoru tries to evacuate with his class, but after one of the villains warps everyone to different areas, Minoru ends up in the flood area along with Izuku and Tsuyu Asui. He is then saved by Tsuyu, who abruptly throws him into a boat after he made an inappropriate comment about her breasts.
Minoru thinks that the villains would never be able to kill All Might and says that he will come to save the day. Tsuyu tells him that the villains would not go through all this trouble if they did not know how to kill him and that they would torture them to death, which frightens Minoru.
