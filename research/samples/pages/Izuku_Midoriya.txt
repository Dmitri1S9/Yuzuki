=== PAGE: Izuku Midoriya ===
== Introduction ==
Izuku Midoriya, also known as the One For All Hero: Deku, is the main protagonist of the My Hero Academia manga and anime series.
Even though Izuku was born Quirkless, he manages to catch the attention of the legendary Hero All Might, due to his innate heroism and a strong sense of justice, and has since become his close pupil, as well as a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School. All Might passed on his transferable Quirk to Izuku, making him the ninth and final wielder of One For All.
Following the Paranormal Liberation War, with Tomura Shigaraki and All For One actively targeting Izuku for his Quirk, he decided to leave U.A. before finishing his first year to keep his classmates out of harm's way. After encouragement and support from his friends, he later returns to the school and leads the Heroes against the Villains in one final battle to save the world. After the Final War, Izuku managed to defeat Tomura and All For One, but at the cost of rendering himself Quirkless once more.
After graduation, Izuku loses the embers of One For All and becomes a teacher at U.A., teaching History. After eight years, advancements in technology allows him to return to active Hero duty, alongside his fellow Class A members, later becoming the No. 4 Pro Hero.

== Appearance ==
Izuku is somewhat short for his age, his round face is framed by a short mop of fluffy dark-green hair which curls up at odd angles around his head, casting noticeably dark shadows onto itself. His eyes are large and somewhat circular and his irises are the same shade of green as his hair, which at times are very watery, and are usually stretched quite wide, giving him an innocent, energized appearance. He has a set of four symmetrical freckles in diamond formations, one on each cheek. Izuku has often been described as "plain-looking" or "not standing out" by others.
Though he was initially very skinny, Izuku has gained toned muscles after his ten months of extensive training with All Might, and as the series progresses, these are shown to become more developed and defined as he gradually grows stronger.
After his battle against Shoto Todoroki, the fingers on his right hand became slightly deformed, and his hand shows visible scarring. His right arm was further damaged after his intense fight with Muscular, leaving many more scars. Following his recovery after the previously mentioned fight, Izuku wears a black compression sleeve on the upper portion of his right arm in order to support the heavy damage it sustained: it is partially visible when he wears short sleeves.
During his time in Aldera , he wore a plain black uniform with yellow buttons, accompanied by his signature bright red high top boots, which have thick white soles and black laces.
At U.A., he wears the standard male uniform: a light gray blazer with dark turquoise trimmings over a white dress shirt and dress pants, along with a red tie which he doesn't do up properly, leaving it much shorter than how it should be. Instead of the plain brown shoes worn by the majority of the student body, he still sports his signature red shoes.
Izuku's first hero costume, known as "Costume Alpha", was made by his mother. It was a light green full-body jumpsuit with a red belt, black elbow pads and knee pads, and white gloves, along with his signature red boots. He also wears a mask with long ear-like protrusions that resembles All Might's haircut, and a respirator with a makeshift smile on it that also reflects that of said hero. The costume was severely damaged as a result of his first battle against Katsuki Bakugo and had to be repaired, so during the U.S.J. Arc and My Hero Academia - Save! Rescue Training, Izuku had to wear the U.A. gym uniform. This attire consists of a dark blue high-collared tracksuit with thick white lines over his torso and down his legs, forming the letters "U" and "A," and along with this, the old respirator, white gloves, and black elbow and knee pads.
His second hero costume, known as "Costume Beta", has a few changes made by the costume designers and now looks sturdier and more combat-ready in appearance. The color is now a darker teal with black outlines instead of white. It includes light beige shoulder pads, black knee pads that have been extended to his legs, now reaching up to his thighs. His new gloves are also tinted light beige and are decorated with two blue stripes. His respirator is now metal, diamond-shaped, and has eight holes. Izuku goes without a mask in his second suit, and although the mask is still present, he doesn't wear it much.
During Class 1-A's week of Ultimate Move Training, Izuku had his costume upgraded again to suit his new fighting style, Shoot Style, by adding iron soles to his red boots to increase the power of his kicks. Arm braces were added, along with wrist straps and extending the blue stripes around his biceps, and both his soles and knee pads have yellow bolts fastened into them (originally added before the upgrade in the manga). He called this collection of upgrades "Costume Gamma".
Izuku's fourth hero costume, known as "Costume Delta", makes some minor alterations from his previous hero costume. Before the U.A. School Festival, Mei Hatsume help creates him support gloves, to better help him use his powers for long-range attacks. Prior to the Joint Training Arc, Izuku adds the gloves to his hero costume and uses them in combat.
For Mission: Humarise, Izuku and his classmates at the Endeavor Agency are given special advanced stealth costumes by the World Heroes Association for their mission at Otheon. Izuku's stealth costume was redesigned based on his Delta Costume, consisting of a black suit with teal-green highlights on the lines, equipped with black iron soles and black boots. This costume also includes two silver plates on his shoulders with the letters "WHA" and two straps coming down from his upper body pads with a red button on the left.
After the Paranormal Liberation War, his costume was damaged, giving it a darker and more unclean look. He also wore Gran Torino's cape, as a memoir to his former mentor and to the deceased Nana Shimura. Due to his arm bracers and gloves being destroyed, he wore four Mid-Gauntlets to support his arms and legs, as well as the old gloves from his beta costume. In addition, Izuku wore his mask and respirator much more frequently in order to conceal his identity. Interestingly, him wearing the mask now casts shadows over his eyes, which causes only his green irises to be visible. This appearing feature is very similar to All Might's look, who has his blue eyes shine through the shadows. This is Izuku's fifth hero costume, known as "Costume Epsilon."
Following his fight with Lady Nagant, Izuku's left arm Mid-Gauntlet is partially destroyed, leaving it practically useless. After the explosion at All For One's mansion in Haibori Woods, Izuku gains an overall monstrous appearance that is stained in blood and filth. His costume, cape, and gloves become more damaged and torn, with the gloves looking more claw-like, his respirator appears to have gathered rust and chipped edges, and his iron soles becoming crooked like talons. In addition to his monstrous appearance, Izuku also constantly sprouts up to six Blackwhip tendrils from his back to always be ready for battle, which Hanta Sero commented was what kept scaring people.
After finally recovering and returning back to U.A., Izuku temporarily wore his U.A. gym uniform, before his Costume Epsilon was eventually rebuilt, accompanied by a new pair of Air Force Gloves attached to make-shift Mid-Gauntlets created by Mei, with a golden stripe along the bicep area, alongside Gran Torino's cape formed as a scarf. The costume has a darker green color, gold outlines instead of black, dark green kneepads, and foregoes the cowl altogether, whilst retaining the damaged respirator from Costume Epsilon. This serves as Izuku's sixth hero costume, known as "Costume Zeta".
During his final battle with Tomura, his respirator and at least one of his Mid-Gauntlets are destroyed. His right arm also ends up covered in black coils as a result of overusing Full Cowl and Blackwhip from within his body to repair his broken tendons. Most of Gran Torino's cape is also ruined, and eventually completely destroyed by Tomura.
In order to deal with Gearshift's recoil, he takes on a form known as Deku Overlay, using Blackwhip to control himself, creating a more savage appearance resembling how he looked post-War. He has four Blackwhip tendrils sprouting from his back, and two more from his torso, his fingers are laced with black claws, and his entire body is snaked with Blackwhip with the mouth resembling a jaw. After forcibly transferring all the Quirks from One For All into Tomura, he drops his Overlay form, reverting himself back to his original appearance. Upon returning to the real world, he loses both of his arms, as they were decayed during his interaction with Tenko in the mental realm. However, due to the effects of Rewind courtesy of Eri's horn, his arms managed to regenerate to just before they were decayed. After slightly recovering, Izuku is given bandages around his wrists and a rare All Might shirt from one of the refugees to wear before joining the final battle. This costume is known as "Costume Origin".
In the aftermath of the Final War, Izuku acquires two new scars, one on the right side of his head, and the other below his right eye spreading down his cheek and reaching his chin, along with both of his hands being more heavily scarred. His hair is also shaved down to an undercut on the right side while recovering from his injuries inflicted by Tomura.
He is also shown wearing a new costume, which is similar to his "Costume Zeta" outfit, aside from some small changes. It keeps the bodysuit to the previous costume, and Izuku now has its cowl and respirator fully repaired. His Air Force Gloves are also fixed and slightly redesigned, and in place of his Mid-Gauntlets is a set of gray, protective wrappings which have elbows pads attached, combing aspects of his old elbow pads and arm bracers. He is also shown not wearing his Iron Soles and Gran Torino's cape, due to both being destroyed by Tomura. This is known as "Costume Eta".
Eventually, Izuku's hair returns back to its original style, and by the time of his graduation, he is shown to now wear it slightly parted to the right.
Eight years later, Izuku has grown taller and more mature. As a teacher at U.A. High, he wears a black suit with a white shirt and green tie, along with some brown business shoes.
Created by Melissa Shield and Mei using data collected from All Might's final fight against All For One; funded by members of Class A; and gifted to him by All Might, Izuku receives a new technology-based Hero costume that looks markedly like his Costume Zeta, albeit more mechanical. It features a long, flowing yellow cape resembling Gran Torino's made from nano machines, jet armor over his legs, boots that resemble his Iron Soles, gauntlets resembling the Shoot Style Gloves, a pouch around his waist, and the same green with orange stripes color scheme as the Costume Zeta. This costume folds itself into a briefcase, which is also colored like the suit, that is marked with the number 18, Izuku's seat number when he was a student.

== Personality ==
Izuku is a very timid, reserved, and polite boy, frequently overreacting to abnormal situations with exaggerated expressions. Due to yeаrs of being looked down on by Katsuki for lacking a Quirk, he is initially portrayed as insecure, tearful, vulnerable, and non-expressive. These traits are especially present around Katsuki, who also constantly harangued him for his aspirations to become a hero. However, after being accepted into U.A., making new friends, and facing Katsuki during the Battle Trial Arc, Izuku gradually matures into a more confident and braver person who's always eager to prove his worth as a hero, eventually developing strong leadership skills, which combined with his passion and strategic abilities, have turned him into a central figure within Class 1-A alongside Katsuki.
Izuku is a quite diligent and strong-willed student, being extremely (and sometimes scarily) enthusiastic about topics related to heroes. His dream drives him to write down notes about everything he learns about heroes' Quirks and fighting capabilities. Thanks to this practice, Izuku has developed a great analytical mind and can form complex battle plans in a few seconds, factoring in the best ways he can utilize the Quirks of allies and enemies alike for his own advantage.
Izuku externalizes his observations through endless mumbling, a habit that annoys or creeps out his peers. Izuku often writes down his observations in a variety of notebooks he titles as Hero Analysis for the Future; he checks on them regularly during school activities, during his free time, or at night.
Izuku is caring and emotional, never hesitating to help or rescue someone in danger, even if he knows that he might not be strong or otherwise qualified enough to do it. Often, he does this on instinct, taking a more careless approach than the usual overthinking he goes through and putting himself in peril in order to protect someone. All Might claims that this trait gives Izuku the most important natural and innate instinct to be a hero; the pure and genuine desire to help people. However, Izuku putting others' lives above his own can go to the extreme, to the point where he has little to no sense of self-preservation. Katsuki speculates that the bullying Izuku received for being Quirkless made him internalize that his life has less worth. Izuku will routinely break his arms for the sake of others, even if it's not completely necessary, such as demanding Shoto to unlock his full potential by using his fire side. This can make Izuku somewhat short-sighted, as he continued to break his arms, despite warnings he could permanently paralyze them and cut his hero career short. Eventually, Izuku relented, adapting his battle style to put less strain on his body. Though when he's in a particularly tight situation, he'll pull back on old habits.
Izuku is known to help or lecture people with personal and emotional problems, regardless of if it's his business or not, claiming that a hero should meddle in other people's lives. This audacious spirit, while initially viewed as perplexing or even insane by others, is also recognized by many, and has earned him the loyalty, gratitude and respect of characters that were previously antagonistic towards him such as Tenya Ida, Shoto, Hitoshi Shinso, Kota Izumi, Sir Nighteye and even the likes of the Hero Killer: Stain and Lady Nagant.
He has a strong fascination with heroes and is shown well-versed in their history, sometimes even surprising the heroes themselves with his vast knowledge of their abilities and past exploits. He is prone to geeking out and rambling on about the heroes he meets, often bringing up their history and accomplishments, while also being eager to share this knowledge with others. Of all heroes, the Symbol of Peace, All Might, has impacted and modeled Izuku's life the most. Many of Izuku's decisions and actions result from his desire to become a Pro Hero similar to All Might, and thus he has a great devotion to him.
Izuku is an avid collector of All Might merchandise, has knowledge of his numerous feats of heroism (no matter how minor), and emulates many of his traits. Having inherited his Quirk, one of Izuku's current priorities is to be able to live up to his idol's legacy, as he is always looking for ways to improve his usage of One For All, being aware of the immense pressure that comes with succeeding the greatest Hero of all time. The unwavering trust and approval Izuku receives from All Might has been vital to his development as a hero, especially when the aforementioned feelings of self-doubt and unworthiness plague his mind.
Izuku appears to have a more brash and abrasive side to him, which typically shows up when he finds himself in combat. This side of him makes him act more like Katsuki, being rather loud and unwavering, and also with a strong drive towards victory. He claims that this is because he sees Katsuki as the embodiment of what someone who strives for victory should be. He has also admitted that he doesn't like this side of him very much and tends to keep it in check. He does, however, find himself giving in to this almost feral rage when in dire situations and faced against opponents who had brutally hurt people he cares for, like in his battles against Overhaul and Tomura.
Izuku also possesses an empathetic side and has developed a desire to want to understand the Villains he has faced, and potentially save them from themselves. While initially believing Tomura to be a madman who just wants to meaninglessly hurt others, after learning about his tragic history and sensing the pain in his heart through their connection between One For All and All For One, this re-invigorates his belief that a great Hero is someone who saves others, not kills, and because of this, desires to learn how they became villains and see if there is any way to prevent a fight, though he does concede he very well may have to kill Tomura. While he is not naïve in this ideology and will resort to violence if there is no other option, like during his second fight with Muscular, he truly wishes to reach out and help villains recover and reform themselves, even giving Kai Chisaki (whom he was still bitter towards) the chance to save his boss should he make amends with Eri. At the same time, Izuku desires to save Tomura from his life of villainy and pain, even when such a notion feels unlikely and is deemed pretentious by others, showcasing his great sense of humanity.
Following the conclusion of the Paranormal Liberation War, Izuku had begun to drastically change his behavior. After suffering from the trauma brought on by the War, seeing countless innocent people have their lives ruined by the current state of society, having All For One relentlessly target him for his Quirk, and bearing the responsibility of protecting his friends, Izuku became more cold and distant towards others. When All For One vowed to break him both physically and mentally, he became increasingly paranoid about how much harm he will bring to those who are around him. The overwhelming stress and burden resulted in him rushing into fights and leaving behind All Might and the other Heroes looking after him so that no one else will get hurt. His disregard for his own well-being becomes the worst it ever has been as well, neglecting to look after his basic needs like sleeping and eating. Katsuki has noted this behavior to be very similar to how All Might was when he was the Symbol of Peace. This increasingly driven, serious side of him has even caused civilians to note that he doesn't look like a hero. After his friends from U.A. get through to him and take him back in, these traits seem to have diminished, with him now being determined in stopping All For One alongside his friends, teachers, and allies.
During the Final War, even after witnessing Katsuki's near demise at the hands of Tomura, Izuku maintains his desire to find a way to save his enemy. In the core of One For All, Kudo recognizes that Izuku's drive to save people is through his belief that everyone possesses an innately human heart, motivating him forward despite suffering grave injuries during his battle against the overwhelmingly powerful Tomura. Once again, Izuku becomes willing to sacrifice One For All as a means of reaching Tomura's origin and finding the source of his pain and hatred, though this would be interrupted by the untimely return of All For One's vestige who seized back control of Tomura's body. With his friends by his side however, Izuku manages to triumph over the Demon Lord, even seeing through All For One's evil persona and recognizing him as a lonely and vulnerable man.
During the war's aftermath, Izuku still holds some regret that he couldn't save Tomura from dying, but was assured by All Might that he managed to save Tomura's soul by vanquishing his pain and that he managed to complete the duty of One For All. After being rendered Quirkless again with only the leftover embers of One For All remaining, Izuku tries to put on a brave front but is visibly distraught over his time as a hero seemingly running out. He's also rather muted when attending U.A. as a second year a week later, and doleful but accepting of Yuga's decision to leave U.A. and start his hero education over. When he tries to strike up a conversation with Ochaco only for her to deflect by commenting on his hair, he also appears concerned for her while uncertain of how to speak with her further.
Izuku also chooses to visit Spinner at Central Hospital to deliver him Tomura's final words; that he fought to the bitter end in order to destroy. Izuku was undeterred when Spinner labelled him as a murderer, only wanting to carry out Tomura's final request, and kept his composure when Spinner grabbed him after activating his Body Bulk Quirk, even stopping the doctor from having the villain restrained. Out of compassion, Izuku tells Spinner that Tomura still cared about him and the rest of the League, and at the end of the visit, he assured Spinner that he would never forget about Tomura. Much later, sensing that Ochaco was hiding something, he goes to comfort her, thanking Ochaco for always helping him and calling her his hero. When Ochaco breaks down and mourns over Himiko's sacrifice, Izuku shares his own guilt over causing Tomura's death, but states that even when times seem unbearable, going above and beyond to reach out and offer help will make a difference. Following this, Izuku accepts that he completed the purpose of One For All, a power meant to help others, and resumes his daily life with his classmates.
Eight years pass and Izuku continues his studies at U.A. while documenting his story and experiences in his notebook, reflecting over how his dream of becoming a hero came true even when it seemed impossible, and that everyone around him became a hero by giving him their hopes, prayers, and encouragement to succeed. Eventually, the embers of One For All fade away and Izuku accepts becoming Quirkless again as he goes on to become a teacher at U.A. High School, admitting that while he misses being a hero, he is content with using his experience to help and encourage others. Izuku has also retained some of his old traits, such as his selfless need to help other people and his mumbling habit, as seen when he flung himself to the ground to save a boy from falling and encounters Dai, telling the middle school student that he can become a hero with his will to help others, even when he possesses an underpowered Quirk. While Izuku was hesitant to accept a mechanized suit as a gift from All Might and Class A, the former Symbol of Peace recognized that Izuku still has the heart of a hero and told him that he had earned it fair and square, causing Izuku to tear up and rejoice as he joins his friends and former classmates, becoming a hero once again.
While being driven by Katsuki and accompanied by Eijiro Kirishima to Class A's reunion party a month later, Izuku reveals that he still intends to carry on teaching while doing hero work and collecting data on his suit, saying that he thinks he would have started teaching even if he kept One For All, as he enjoys sharing his knowledge with others. However, having lived his life consistently putting others before himself, with little to no regard for his own well being and personal happiness, Izuku was told by Katsuki to think more highly of himself, being further warned that giving everyone special treatment meant that no one is truly special to him. During Class A's dinner party, Izuku listens on as his friends discuss the topic of heroes now having more free time to spend pursuing hobbies and desires outside of heroism, such as dating, and, in Shoto's case, bowl and chopstick making classes. Left in deep thought, Izuku contemplates on whether he too wanted more out of his own life despite considering himself to already be "blessed enough", ultimately leading him to fully realize his feelings for Ochaco. Following through on Katsuki's earlier advice, Izuku later finds Ochaco to openly confess his feelings, now wanting to spend more time together with her going forward, and is left overjoyed upon learning that their feelings for one another are mutual, as the two happily take each other's hand to mark the start of a new relationship.
Shortly upon his return to hero work, Izuku would go on to achieve tremendous feats and is welcomed back to the hero world at large with thunderous applause by everyone who remembers him, being officially called the One For All Hero: Deku, and even skyrocketing up to the rank of No. 4 Hero in Japan.

== Abilities ==
Overall Abilities: Izuku's greatest asset prior to receiving his Quirk was his vast knowledge of fundamental hero skills and tactics. Izuku studied Pro Heroes for years and is able to apply that knowledge during crisis situations in a practical manner. His immense bravery and desire to become a hero were also important factors in giving him the mentality to protect others. Before gaining his Quirk from All Might, he took on the Sludge Villain and rescued Katsuki because of his boldness and application of heroic skills, something that deeply impressed the No. 1 Hero.
Upon training with All Might, Izuku inherited One For All from him and was granted the ability to harness a stockpile of powerful energy. He can move faster than the eye can see and take down an enormous Villain Bot in a single punch, but the physical backlash at the time prevented Izuku from being able to fight properly or compete with his peers on an even playing field, as throwing a punch or kick with anything beyond moderate force will shatter the bones in his arms and legs. Even so, he still possessed impressive physical durability, and his drive allowed him to withstand the drawbacks of One For All for the most part.
Despite the intense drawbacks of One For All, Izuku's wits provide him with ways to use One For All that is still effective in battle. He also retains his ability to act instinctively based on his notes and possesses basic hand-to-hand combat knowledge. By applying those correctly, Izuku is able to fight toe-to-toe with even the strongest students of Class 1-A, such as Katsuki and Shoto.
Under the tutelage of Gran Torino, Izuku acquired more control over One For All, leading to the creation of Full Cowl, a technique that would eventually become a key component of his fighting style. With Izuku gaining greatly enhanced maneuverability, strength, and a significant reduction in the physical backlash created when using his Quirk uncontrollably, he was able to drastically improve his overall fighting capabilities; his newly attained abilities garnered praise from Gran Torino, who noted that Izuku was on a higher level than he was compared to the Sports Festival. This specific improvement enabled Izuku to compete more closely with his classmates in the physical aspects of the Hero Course and even allows him to fight properly against top villains like the Hero Killer: Stain, who had single-handedly bested various Pro Heroes.
As Izuku's mastery over his Quirk improves, so do his practical skills. Also, when his skills and experience increase, so does his confidence, which is said to be a source of inspiration to his classmates as well. His body, however, still remains relatively vulnerable to One For All's drawbacks, which gives Izuku a disadvantage against enemies like Muscular or Overhaul, whose Quirks were proven to be too powerful against One For All's weaker outputs, forcing scenarios where Izuku had to sacrifice his body in order to go all out. In order to protect his own body, particularly his arms, Izuku eventually develops his own unique fighting style, diverging himself further from All Might's.
Currently, Izuku is acknowledged by his classmates as a strong, reliable, leading figure, with both his strength and intelligence being respected equally. However, Izuku's Quirk is still an enigma for them (including Izuku himself), which makes him an unpredictable ally for better or worse. As Izuku keeps growing, so does One For All, and with the prospect of having new abilities unlocked in the future, Izuku has to work even harder in order to fully dominate the Quirk that was passed to him.
By the time of the Paranormal Liberation War, Izuku's skills have grown exponentially to the point that he is able to fight alongside exceptional Pro Heroes such as Eraser Head, Gran Torino, and even the current number one Pro Hero, Endeavor. But his biggest feat thus far is holding his own against the Grand Commander of the Liberation Front, Tomura Shigaraki, who has inherited All For One and has a modified body with strength comparable to All Might, and forcing the all-powerful villain on the defensive despite the latter easily overpowering the top Pro Heroes.
Following the aftermath of the Paranormal Liberation War, Izuku has displayed even greater mastery over his Quirks and showcased the ability to easily outmaneuver his enemies with Blackwhip and 45% of One For All, detect danger without gaining sensory overload, prove En wrong by creating a new Ultimate Move using Smokescreen, and easily alternate between his arsenal of Quirks in his fights with Muscular and Lady Nagant. In his fight against Class 1-A, Izuku displayed himself to be easily capable of fighting on even terms with some of U.A. High School's most powerful students and was shown capable of completely dominating them even when severely outnumbered before succumbing to exhaustion and stress.
During the course of the Final War, Izuku has improved over the span of a month, where he is the only one who has a chance at defeating the fully powered Tomura, and in their final encounter, he has achieved equal standing with Tomura in terms of strength and speed despite the latter changing into his final form. Later in the fight, Izuku demonstrates a level of immense power that he is capable of annihilating Tomura's fingers and arm, with Tomura even acknowledging Izuku could finish him off with a single full powered blow, but since he refuses to kill him, is forced on the defensive instead.
Trained Muscles: Usage of One For All requires the user to have strong muscles, as One For All is a lot to handle and could cause the user's body to be blown apart if they don't possess the necessary bodily strength. Having been trained by All Might for 10 months in order to get his body physically ready to inherit One For All, Izuku gained a muscular physique that lets him perform at parameters higher than that of an average student outside U.A.'s hero course. During the Quirk Apprehension Test, he demonstrated a grip strength of 56 kilograms and the ability to run the 50-meter dash in 7.02 seconds (15.93 mph/25.64 kph). Even after these events, Deku diligently trains his muscles every day and becomes physically stronger throughout the series. He is often seen running laps and using dumbbells or grippers to train during his free time. Deku can also be observed doing the air chair exercise during the breaks between classes.
Immense Stamina: Izuku has shown on many occasions that he is capable of handling large amounts of pain. Such times were mostly side-effects of overusing his Quirk and frequently led to various broken bones. Izuku is able to avoid those repercussions by staying in a safe range of One For All power output (currently, this is around 45%).
Should he be forced to output more power, Izuku is still capable of fighting decently even with broken limbs thanks to the effects of adrenaline, but will succumb to exhaustion a while later. Izuku also has to be wary of his arms, which have grown damaged due to his repeated injuries and could eventually stop working altogether if he doesn't restrain himself.
After multiple months of hard work and training with the No. 1 Pro Hero Endeavor, Izuku has become much sturdier and resilient when it comes to the drawbacks of his Quirk, allowing him to withstand the recoil from 100% attacks without being instantly incapacitated. He has also shown an even greater pain threshold, when he managed to endure multiple smash attacks at 100% and attacks from Tomura while still standing.
Izuku has shown to even be capable of operating for days on-end without eating or sleeping, going across Japan and fighting multiple villains. Even when severe sleep deprivation and physical exhaustion accumulated, Izuku still fought against powerful villains for nearly a month and made it very difficult for his former classmates to restrain him for a lengthy period of time before finally collapsing. By the time of the Gollini Invasion, it's implied that Izuku's efforts were so militant back then, that it's the reason Villains began nicknaming him "The Jailbreaker Hunter" and recognizing the boy off the color of his costume alone. Izuku was also able to withstand overusing Gearshift after its five minute limit was up, though had to wait several minutes to cool down or face complete loss of breath.
Later in his final battle with Tomura, Izuku showed to be capable of battling the villain even when experiencing near paralyzing pain and being unable to breathe properly due to the recoil of Gearshift. After All for One regains control over Tomura’s body, Izuku proves able to continue fighting the villain even with all the damage accumulated earlier in the battle and only using the remaining embers of One for All.
Immense Strength: Even without his Quirk, Izuku has shown high levels of physical strength for a person his age. During his training with All Might (who weighs 255 kilograms or 562 pounds), he was shown to be able to lift him on his back while walking. During the sports festival, he could lift a slab of metal roughly the size of his body and wield it as an effective weapon to cleave a one-point bot in two. Demonstrably, using One For All at steadily higher percentages ends up enhancing his body to superhuman levels in order to be capable of doing so.
When using One For All, his strength further increases to a variety of boundless levels: at 5%, he can shatter metal pillars with a single punch, at 8%, he can lift a steel I beam one-handed. At 15%, he could lift several vehicles in the air with the aid of Blackwhip. At 30%, he can lift a bus full of people one-handed and run fast enough to evade Tomura's decay and restrain the enhanced Tomura with aid from Blackwhip and his anger.
During the Dark Hero Arc, Izuku's strength increases even further and he can now use 45% as his regular output with no backlash. Using 45%, he could defeat a weakened Muscular with a single punch when he was unsuccessful before, casually collapse a building when fighting his classmates, and even managed to break out of Fumikage Tokoyami's Ragnarök, shocking his friends.
At 100%, Izuku's strength surpasses even the level of All Might, this allows him to commit astonishing feats. He could easily destroy a massive Villain Bot with a single punch, annihilate a twelve story building, create torrents of wind pressure with a flick of his fingers, and create a massive explosion with a single punch.
With the Second User's Quirk, Gearshift, and the Third User's Fa Jin combined, he can make his punches even faster and build immense kinetic energy feedback, inflicting catastrophic damage to "Tomura" during the Final War, as each punch breaks the sound barrier, to the point Mirio notices the delayed sound of the punches before it can be heard. This combination increases his strength from his 100% limit to what is described as 120% in power. Tomura admits that if Izuku landed a direct attack without holding anything back, even his body wouldn't be able to regenerate.
In the final battle against All For One, despite only possessing the remaining embers of One For All, he was still the only one capable of harming the villain. With his final strike, he made his greatest feat of strength yet when he not only destroyed Tomura's crumbling body and reduced it to ashes, but also blew away the massive storm created by Shoto, Endeavor, and Dabi, clearing the weather of not only all of Japan, but also across America. This final attack was also so powerful that America was still experiencing strong winds days after it happened.
Afterwards, Izuku is also shown to be capable of easily lifting debris and rubble despite not using his remaining One For All embers. Even compared to the pros, Izuku demonstrates a high degree of bodily strength and keen insight into how to leverage it best that isn't tied to any Quirk.
Immense Speed and Reflexes: Izuku possesses great speed and reflexes with and without Full Cowl. He can react in time to defend from Shoto's ice which has been shown to occur in the literal blink of an eye and can keep up with Katsuki's movements in their first fight.
When using Full Cowl, his reflexes and movement speed elevates to higher levels; at 5%, he can keep up with Tenya's Recipro Burst which can move faster than the eye can see, at 8%, he proved to surpass Katsuki's own speed and quickly and easily overwhelmed Hitoshi in combat in spite of Hitoshi's improvements, at 20%, he could outpace Overhaul and at 30%, he can keep up with the enhanced Tomura's speed. At 45%, he was capable of keeping up with Lady Nagant's bullet and was even capable of dodging several of her shots, as well as keeping up with Muscular's movements which he previously could not follow.
At 100%, his speed rises to levels rivaling or even surpassing that of All Might, which allows him to casually blitz capable villains and move several times faster than the eye can see. During his fight with Overhaul, he could completely outspeed the villain and move fast enough to create afterimages, and later completely overwhelm him with an instantaneous rush of punches. When fighting against Tomura Shigaraki, he could effortlessly blitz the villain and severely damage his weakened body, despite him possessing speed rivaling that of All Might and outclassing that of Gran Torino.
With his Faux 100%, Izuku's speed is on par with All Might's in his prime. Izuku overtook a already fired and moving sniper bullet from Lady Nagant. This allowed him to reach and push Overhaul aside, even before the bullet was able to finish the last small part of the entire distance. With this technique, his speed is so great that, even in an exhausted state, it took the combined effort of Class-A just to catch up with him.
After Izuku unlocked and mastered the Second User of One For All's Quirk, Gearshift, his speed is incredibly enhanced, enabling him to leave the sound of his attacks far behind his movements. His speed has increased so drastically that every punch he makes delayed a sound before it can be heard. "Tomura" stated that even his eyes are completely incapable of keeping up with him at this speed. More impressively, Izuku is shown to be able to react to Tomura in a millisecond and cut his fingers off the instant they touched his face before his Decay could activate. However, after his Danger Sense was stolen, Izuku's reflexes have taken a sharp downward spiral, but was still able to react to Tomura's onslaught of attacks that blasts him all the way to Mt. Fuji.
Immense Durability: Izuku has shown the ability to withstand explosions from Katsuki without One For All and with 5% he's taken building-sized explosions from Katsuki and attacks from Muscular. At 30% of his power, he's withstood blows from the enhanced Tomura, who is said to have power rivalling All Might himself. At 45%, he withstood being shot by Lady Nagant and even managed to survive a massive explosion triggered by All For One. Upon facing his classmates, Izuku was capable of withstanding several powerful attacks such as Fumikage's Ragnarök, ramming full-speed into Shoto's Heaven-Piercing Ice Wall, and even withstanding a collapsing building with little to no injuries.
During Izuku's fight with Flect Turn, he showed to be capable of withstanding many of his own smash attacks being reflected against him and sending him crashing through the surrounding area multiple times. Later in the fight, he survived being shot by multiple lasers and lost a significant amount of blood, yet he continued to battle the villain fiercely despite his injuries.
At 100%, Izuku's durability and endurance rise to extreme levels, making him appear virtually invulnerable to physical trauma. In his fight against Tomura Shigaraki, he could withstand several attacks from the PLF leader and his own Quirk's backlash. In his fight against Nine, Izuku proved easily capable of completely nullifying several of the villain's attacks. However, Izuku's durability has shown to depend on the amount of injuries he has sustained, with minimal injuries providing the greatest results.
In his final battle with Tomura, Izuku showed to be capable of receiving a powerful punch from the villain with no injuries when the villain’s quirks were erased. His durability and endurance increase further when he uses blackwhip to shield himself from Tomura’s attacks after he regained his quirks, using it internally and externally to withstand the major damage and avoid lethal wounds.
Keen Intellect: Izuku has proven to be exceptionally intelligent on many occasions, displaying both keen observational and analytical skills as well as sharp intuition and resourcefulness. His grades are among the highest of his class, showing that his One For All training rarely interferes with his studying. Izuku's intelligence has probably been a more useful asset to him than One For All so far. Izuku is a quick learner, which has helped him figure out most of One For All's mechanisms in a short span of time and work around its limitations. While some of his moves have been directly inspired by All Might (if not, outright copied), the majority of Izuku's moves and techniques were all conceived by him during sudden sparks of inspiration, proof of his creativity.
Izuku also has extensive knowledge of Pro Heroes, his classmates, and their Quirks, taking notes of them in great detail in his Hero Analysis for the Future. This is fruit of excellent observational skills coupled with his own desires in becoming a hero by learning from the best, meaning that Izuku always has a natural interest in expanding his knowledge further and further.
Izuku is an exceptional strategist and tactician. By combining his aforementioned quick learning and knowledge about Quirks, Izuku is able to devise plans that take advantage of his friends' abilities to the fullest. Izuku's calculations have been crucial at several moments of his school life, allowing him to earn victories, such as in the Battle Trial; escape dangerous situations, such as the U.S.J. Incident; and save lives, such as in the Vanguard Action Squad Invasion and Kamino Incident. Indeed, in his battle with his classmates, he used his knowledge of their powers to his advantage and easily overcame a majority of them, shocking a few of them with his skill.
His intelligence is greatly respected by everyone in Class 1-A as many of his peers look up to him for planning and follow his strategies to the bottom line. Even Momo Yaoyorozu, the smartest student in the class, has looked up to Izuku as a reference.
Adaptability: Thanks to his analytical abilities, Izuku is able to replicate the techniques of others. For example, after training with Gran Torino and gaining minor mastery of One For All, Izuku was able to duplicate Katsuki's moves during a training exercise. After training and drawing inspiration from his classmate, Tsuyu Asui, Izuku has become proficient enough with Blackwhip to generate a dark tendril from his tongue, allowing him to replicate her "Froppy Style" combat.
After unlocking more assimilated Quirks from One For All, Izuku can craft and combine some of the Quirks to make custom Ultimate Moves such as Blackchain, Faux 100 Percent, and Faux 100 Percent: Manchester Smash.
Furthermore, during his final battle with Tomura Shigaraki, upon becoming immobilized from the use of Gearshift, he used Blackwhip to flex and extend his muscles manually from within, allowing him to build up Fa Jin and swing his arm.
Leadership Skills: As a natural consequence of his bravery and strategic mindset, Izuku has displayed incredible leadership skills on various occasions.
By the start of his U.A. life, Izuku had already earned the trust of Ochaco Uraraka and Tenya thanks to his actions in the U.A. Entrance Exam, which was enough to allow him to be voted class representative early on, although he relinquished the position to Tenya shortly after. Tsuyu and Minoru Mineta also placed their faith on Izuku during the U.S.J. Incident, following his instructions with little hesitation. By the time of the First Term Final Exam, he had already fought alongside Katsuki and Shoto, who were previously antagonistic to him.
Izuku might not be aware of this, but he is one of the main motivators within his class alongside Katsuki, thanks to a combination of his heroic drive and practical intellect.
Indomitable Will: Throughout the series, and as one of his greatest traits, Izuku displayed an extraordinary level of willpower. Following his dream to become the greatest hero, Izuku develops great determination and willpower. While feeble and doubtful at the beginning, Izuku steadily developed and proved his strong willpower against far menacing adversaries, people doubting and believing in him, and proven to be stronger than many of his classmates. He was the first one to move his body after All For One induced great fear onto him and his classmates. Even when Sir Nighteye foresaw Izuku's death and Eri's failed rescue, he refused to give up and promised to change fate. He has constantly displayed the ability to never give up no matter how severe the injury. When fighting Overhaul, Izuku continuously steeled his resolve to save and protect Eri. Even after being severely injured and feeling the drawbacks of using One For All at 20%, he still kept on fighting against the villain until going all-out with 100%.
During the Paranormal Liberation War, Izuku displayed his resistance against Tomura's terrifying aura. While he did hallucinate his death for a moment, he quickly recovered from it and showed no fear when confronting the villain. When all hope seemed lost, Izuku decided to do everything in his disposal to stop Tomura, no matter what would happen to himself afterwards and knowingly going for the risk of forever losing his arms or worse. Even after shattering his arms and legs and horrifically injuring himself, he developed the Froppy Style with Blackwhip after being inspired by Tsuyu Asui and desperately attempted to capture Tomura.
After the Paranormal Liberation War, Yo Shindo notices the change and development of Izuku's willpower, stating that he gives off a totally different vibe. Even when Izuku went to confront All For One in the Haibori Woods mansion, he only showed his anger-filled eyes and determination of defeating him. During the Dark Hero Arc, Izuku's will takes a large toll as he begins to succumb to the stress and trauma of the war, as well as leaving U.A. He began to over-exert himself, under-eat, and constantly fend off villains, while showing extremely worrying behavior. He eventually decides to leave behind All Might in order to track down the PLF himself. In reality, Izuku chose to commit these actions to protect his friends from anymore harm, not wanting more people to get hurt or killed due to his actions. This behavior is further enforced after the horrific disfigurement of Lady Nagant right in front of his eyes, which further increased his rage and hatred towards All For One. Eventually, after being rescued by his friends, having Katsuki Bakugo apologize to him for his actions, and Ochaco Uraraka publicly defend him, he finally leaves behind this depressive state and steels his resolve to fight against the villains.
By the time of the Final War, even though Izuku becomes outmatched by Tomura's power, with One For All vulnerable to be stolen and the villain on the verge of destroying Mt. Fuji, Izuku still won't give up and stands against Tomura with no sign of fear. This trait is why Kudo was strongly opposing Izuku as the successor of One For All at first, not because he's a peace-seeking or his desire to save one's archenemy to be a delusional madness, but because of his strong driven notion to save anyone, whether the most vile among them or the Quirkless, as each one of them as an innately human heart. His willpower was so motivated that even with his muscles being torn and suffering through Gearshift's recoil backlash, Izuku still keeps on fighting through. Even within the realm of One For All, and All For One, Izuku's extraordinary willpower was so strong that he was able to withstand against Tomura's immense hatred inside of his core, and make Tomura bend to his will in the final battle. Even after losing his arms, and All For One taking back control of Tomura, Izuku refused to give up fighting. Despite losing his Quirk with only the last embers of One For All, he still manages to turn the spark into a raging inferno similar to All Might's, as he was able to push himself to the brink and dealt the finishing blow to destroy All For One's vestige once and for all, at the cost of ending Tomura's life, with the villain conceding in defeat before succumbing to his injuries and dies as his body disintegrates into ashes.

== Former Quirks ==
One For All: Transferred to him from Toshinori Yagi, Izuku's Quirk allowed him to stockpile an enormous amount of raw power, allowing him to significantly enhance all of his physical abilities to various boundless levels. This resulted in unbelievable levels of strength, speed, stamina, agility, and durability. When Izuku activated One For All, red, vein-like lines course throughout the empowered part of his body.
Izuku could focus the stockpiled power into a single body part, or spread it across his entire body evenly, though, focusing the power in a single part puts a greater strain on that part of his body. He was also able to control what percentage of his full power that he uses. Since he was not born with this power, his body was not naturally suited to handle it, however, over time and with continued training with it, his body has become more accustomed to bracing for the strain. He was able to handle 45% of his full power without breaking his bones, with 30% being his normal output.
In addition to the stockpiling power, Izuku also had access to a subconscious realm where vestiges of the consciousnesses of the previous One For All holders dwell. These vestiges could trigger Izuku to activate One For All without any conscious input from himself, as seen when he was under the control of Hitoshi's Brainwashing Quirk and illusionary shadows of these echoes appeared in his vision. Izuku had little to no control over this aspect of One For All, being unable to freely enter or exit, his body not being fully materialized, which limited his movement within the realm, and only being able to talk through muffled sounds since his mouth has not fully manifested yet. However, Izuku had reached the point where he can freely communicate with the vestiges of the previous users while still conscious. Izuku is the first holder of One For All known to have gained access to this realm while still alive.
Izuku was granted access to the Quirks of the previous owners of One For All, but he has since transferred all of them to Tomura in an effort to defeat him. In addition to transferring the Quirks of One For All, Izuku also transferred One For All itself to Tomura, leaving him with only the "leftover embers".
Gearshift: The Quirk of the second user, Kudo. This Quirk allows Izuku to change the speed and/or velocity of any target he touches, including himself. This allows him to greatly accelerate his own speed and/or the velocity of his target, and enhance the impact force of his own strikes. After years of being empowered within One For All, Gearshift has evolved into an unusual power that cannot be wielded the usual way. It has also been designated as a last resort. Izuku forcibly transferred this Quirk to Tomura so that he can defeat him from the inside of his soul.
Fa Jin: The Quirk of the third user, Bruce. This Quirk allows Izuku to build up and store kinetic energy by performing repetitive movement. He can later release this stored energy as an explosive burst of speed and power.Izuku forcibly transferred this Quirk to Tomura so that he can defeat him from the inside of his soul.
Danger Sense: The Quirk of the fourth user, Hikage Shinomori. Formerly residing in One For All, this Quirk allowed Izuku to detect threats in the surrounding area. Due to the amplification of One For All, the detection caused a stabbing sensation in his head and took a toll on his body. This Quirk was stolen by Tomura during the Final War.
Blackwhip: The Quirk of the fifth user, Daigoro Banjo. This Quirk allows Izuku to generate tendrils of black energy from his body that are good for grappling. It is useful for grabbing things from long distances, capturing opponents, and increasing Izuku's mobility.Izuku forcibly transferred this Quirk to Tomura so that he can defeat him from the inside of his soul.
Smokescreen: The Quirk of the sixth user, En Tayutai. This Quirk allows Izuku to generate a thick cloud of purple smoke from his body. This smoke can cover a very wide area and obscure peoples' vision. Izuku forcibly transferred this Quirk to Tomura so that he can defeat him from the inside of his soul.
Float: The Quirk of the seventh user, Nana Shimura. This Quirk allows Izuku to suspend himself in mid-air, granting him flight. Izuku forcibly transferred this Quirk to Tomura so that he can defeat him from the inside of his soul.

== Ultimate Moves ==
Detroit Smash: The same as All Might's move, except Izuku does it in the form of an uppercut or a straightforward punch.
Detroit Smash: Quintuple: Izuku enhances himself with Gearshift and performs five Detroit Smashes in quick succession.
Delaware Smash: Izuku flicks his finger to create a powerful wind pressure shock wave that has enough force to break large chunks of ice made by Shoto. Due to using this attack at 100%, it fractures the finger, limiting the time he can use this move without gravely injuring himself up to ten times.
United States of World Smash: This Ultimate Move is Izuku's variation of All Might's United States of Smash. Izuku begins this Ultimate Move by increasing his power levels using Full Cowl - 100% and starts unleashing a violent volley of punches. After this, he runs around his opponent, building up momentum and increasing air pressure before delivering a devastating kick. This was first used against Flect Turn.
Full Cowl: Through his training with Gran Torino, Izuku was able to unlock this stage of his power and get better control of his Quirk. This technique allows Izuku to utilize a percentage of the full power of One For All throughout his body, by letting the power course and flow through him instead of concentrating it in one location. According to Izuku, this technique gives him greater maneuverability. Activating this technique gives him immense strength, speed, mobility, and agility. It also prevents him from breaking his bones when attacking. The cost of using this technique reduces the damage of how much his attacks do since instead of centering the power into a certain area, Izuku spreads it throughout his body. Izuku has been steadily improving the upper limits of the percentage of One For All that he could safely use with this technique since its creation. Izuku's initial limits at the time of creating the technique only allowed him to utilize 5% of the maximum output. During Izuku's fight against Katsuki after the Provisional Hero License Exam, he further improved this limit to 8%. Since his battle against Overhaul, Izuku was capable of using 20% without breaking his bones. At the start of Izuku's internship with Endeavor, he described his percentage limitations to be in the interval of 10% to 15% of his full power without any extraneous load and had also stated that he could use 20%, but not without causing immense pain that would majorly hinder his mobility in battle. By the start of the Paranormal Liberation War, Izuku improved this limit to 30% with the extraneous usage limit of 45% of his full power. When Izuku activates Full Cowl, red, vein-like lines course throughout his body that coincides with the energy flowing through him. When fully active, his body and eyes start to glow with a green, bio-electric aura.
5% Detroit Smash: Izuku activates Full Cowl at 5% then jumps into the air and delivers an enhanced downward punch to the opponent. He has also used this move in the form of a straight, forward-facing punch.
Double Detroit Smash: Izuku and All Might combine their Detroit Smashes into a single, devastating charge. They performed this final attack against Wolfram, shattering through a humongous chunk of metal and causing a huge explosion that destroyed Izuku's Full Gauntlet.
Delaware Smash Air Force: Izuku uses his upgraded gloves that Mei made for him to direct a blast of compressed air against his foe, possessing enough concussive force to stun Gentle Criminal while he was in mid-air, giving Izuku an opening to catch up to him. By using all five of his fingers, he can launch up to four Delaware Smashes simultaneously. It requires 20% of his power to use it effectively using One For All.
One For All - 30%: A technique that allows Izuku to use 30% of One For All in his right arm. This percentage was used in conjunction with Melissa Shield's Full Gauntlet, which nullified any drawbacks to using One For All at higher percentages. Because of this, it is unknown how this state affects Izuku's body under normal conditions. With 30% Izuku was able to effortlessly blow away security bots with vast amounts of wind pressure created by the punch.
One For All - 45%: A technique that allows Izuku to use 45% of One For All. He's only able to bring this level of power out for a brief period of time; to keep it from wrecking his body he activates it just before the moment of impact.
St. Louis Smash Air Force: Izuku arches his leg back and performs a strong vertical roundhouse kick, firing a large blast of compressed air.
One For All - 100%: A technique that allows Izuku to attack using the full power of One For All in the desired part of his body, with the cost of said body part breaking upon execution. Further usage of the same limb after an initial attack can cause scarring, as seen with his fights against Shoto (causing Izuku to get surgery on his right hand due to injuries) and Muscular (leaving multiple scars across his right arm).
100% Detroit Smash: This move involves Izuku activating One For All at 100% in his arm and then striking his target with a lunging punch.
1,000,000% Delaware Detroit Smash: This move is the strongest smash move in Izuku's arsenal, which involves him activating One For All at 100% during an adrenaline rush and then striking his opponent with a full-swing attack. When performing the move Izuku first uses a full-handed Delaware Smash, then delivers a devastating Detroit Smash. During the climax of his battle with Muscular, Izuku managed to overpower and launch his augmented opponent with enough force to create an impact crater in solid rock with the strike alone, thus defeating the villain.
Texas Smash: Izuku throws a punch in order to create wind pressure in order to blow objects and people away. This move is first used to critically damage Tomura during the Paranormal Liberation War.
Wyoming Smash: Izuku draws out 100% of his power into his arms, then proceeds to spike his opponent on the head, creating a powerful shockwave as a result. This technique was first used against Tomura during the Paranormal Liberation War.
Full Cowl - Shoot Style: While using Full Cowl, Izuku uses a fighting style that focuses more on kicking. Izuku concentrates One for All: Full Cowl's effects in his torso and legs rather than his arms, allowing him to put out even more power due to the fact that the human leg muscles are naturally stronger than the human arm muscles, granting him enough power and stability and allowing him to step away from the shackles of having to imitate All Might.
St. Louis Smash: While using the Shoot Style, Izuku leaps into the air and before swinging around to deliver a roundhouse kick to the opponent's face. Izuku used this move to incapacitate Gentle Criminal, who had been empowered by La Brava's Lover Mode Quirk to match Izuku's use of One for All: Full Cowl at 8% of its power.
Full Cowl - 20%: Izuku uses 20% of One For All's full power and distributes it throughout his body, giving him even greater power and speed boost.
Manchester Smash: Izuku leaps into the air and flips forward to bring down an axe kick upon his opponent. Although Kai Chisaki managed to evade it, the attack itself pulverized the ground thoroughly enough that Kai wasn't able to completely retaliate with his Overhaul Quirk.
Full Cowl - 30%: A technique that allows Izuku to consistently use 30% of One For All. He was able to utilize this level of One For All near the end of his first year of high school, during the war with the Paranormal Liberation Front.
Full Cowl - 100%: Izuku activates One For All to its fullest extent throughout Full Cowl, granting him a tremendous boost in speed and strength that not only rivaled that of All Might but allowed Izuku to easily overwhelm the likes of Kai Chisaki. This was first used against Kai Chisaki to protect Eri. The power increase seemingly gives him fire-like trails coming from his eyes and light-colored hair. He can currently only use it infinitely with no damage done to his body while in physical contact with Eri. Since Eri has the ability to Rewind the state of a person's body, any damage done to Izuku's body by using One For All at 100% would continually be undone by Eri's Quirk which proved useful in his fight with Overhaul.
Froppy Style: Using Blackwhip, he shoots a singular tendril out of his mouth and uses it like Tsuyu does with her tongue.
Full Blast: Izuku uses Smokescreen at its maximum, producing a wide area of smoke, blocking the view of his opponent and potentially himself, as well.
Pinpoint Focus: Using Blackwhip, he quickly shoots a long-ranged tendril at a specific target to attack them.
Faux 100 Percent: Izuku draws out 45% of One For All's power, and combines it with the stored kinetic energy generated by Fa Jin. By doing this he is able to draw out the blinding speed of using One For All at 100%, without the recoil that comes with it.
Faux 100 Percent: Manchester Smash: Izuku activates Faux 100 Percent, and lands an axe kick on to his opponent at blinding speed.
Blackchain: By combining Blackwhip and Fa Jin, Izuku is able to create a chain that is able to ensnare and launch opponents at great distances.
Transmission: Izuku rushes towards his opponent and punches them several times, with each punch increasing the speed of Gearshift affecting his opponent. These shifts in speed are labelled as "gears."
Overdrive: Izuku uses Gearshift and Fa Jin on himself, increasing his speed and boosting his power to catch up to opponents and unleash powerful attacks on them. The force he unleashes is described as 120% of One For All.
Deku Overlay: After becoming immobilized by the effects of Gearshift, Izuku uses his Blackwhip to pull at the muscles and tendons within his body to simulate movement, which shows up as dark lines underneath his skin.

== Trivia ==
Izuku was originally planned to be an adult as seen in the one-shot My Hero.
In the early concept stages of My Hero Academia, Izuku was known as "Mikumo Akatani". He had a longer and curlier hairdo, with bangs blocking one of his eyes. He was Quirkless in this concept, relying more on makeshift gadgets and luck to deal with obstacles. His hero costume was also slightly more intimidating. The costume was shown in a sketch having an orange color scheme.
Kohei Horikoshi once commented that he was unsatisfied with Mikumo's design, especially his hair. This made Horikoshi think up the current design with slightly shorter hair. He also received suggestions from editors to give the protagonist an actual superpower, so he wouldn't naturally fade into the background.
According to Izuku's profile in Volume 1:
His favorite food is Katsudon.
He was deliberately designed to look plain.
Regarding Izuku's academic life and performance, his U.A. data is as follows:
Student No. 18 in Class 1-A.
Ranked seventh during the U.A. Entrance Exam.
Ranked twentieth (last) for the Quirk Apprehension Test.
His fifty-meter dash record is 7.02 seconds. His previous record was 7.49 in middle school.
His grip strength record is 56 kilograms (123.459 pounds). His previous record was 40 kilograms (88.185 pounds) in middle school.
His softball throw record is 705.3 meters (2313.98 feet). Without his Quirk, it's 46 meters (150 feet).
Ranked fourth in Class 1-A's mid-term grades.
Ranked in the Top 8 of the U.A. Sports Festival First-Year Stage.
Izuku received only one nomination from Gran Torino for his U.A. Sports Festival performance, technically making him the tenth most nominated Class 1-A student.
Scored seventy-one in the Provisional Hero License Exam.
Initially, Izuku was voted to be 1-A's class representative before he decided to willingly pass the position over to Tenya.
Izuku's surname contains the kanji for "green" and "valley", and his given name contains "coming out/being from a certain place, one's turn to go on" and "long ago, old story".
"久" is also homophonous to "nine", which refers to him being the ninth user of One For All.
"Deku" is another reading of his first name, Izuku. Written with different kanji, it can refer to a wooden figure or puppet, which is a term often used as an insult for someone who can't do or achieve anything.
Izuku's nickname, "Deku," was given to him by Katsuki when they were children as an insult.
According to Kohei Horikoshi, the inspiration for the name Deku was from Kenji Miyazawa's poem "" (Be Not Defeated by the Rain).
Later, Ochaco finds the nickname inspiring and says that "Deku" gives off a feeling of "I can do it", because of its similarity to the word meaning "to be able to do".
In the Funimation dub of Episode 1, Katsuki implies that his nickname is an abbreviation of a different insult: "Defenseless Izuku," hence Deku.
In that version of the anime, the scenes that directly discuss the nickname's Japanese definition are typically written around, though it's still referenced roundaboutly in relevant dialogue (i.e. the flashback of Katsuki creating the nickname in Episode 7, and grafting the insult on to the name instead of plainly stating its definition.)

== Inko Midoriya ==
Inko is Izuku's mother, and she cares deeply for him; when her son finds out he's Quirkless and therefore cannot be a hero, she apologizes profusely as if it were her fault. It is hinted that she blames his sadness on herself and has gained weight as a result, but it is unknown if this is true.
However, once Izuku receives his new Quirk, One For All, and gets into his dream school, U.A. High School, she is happy for his newfound powers, but feels guilty about her former beliefs. Inko tries to make it up to him and show her support by creating a costume for Izuku, using the design she had seen him sketch. She brings up her past actions and apologizes for not believing that he could be a hero, reassuring Izuku that she will support him all the way on his path to accomplish his dream; Izuku is touched by this and thanks her.
Despite these words of encouragement, it is evident that Inko becomes worried for Izuku when she sees the distressing amount of damage done to his body after he used his new Quirk. Indeed, after the ordeal at the training camp, Inko is completely against him resuming his studies at U.A. due to the staff's inability to properly protect her son. Izuku is shocked at this and cannot seem to say anything to shift his mother's firm stance. Only after Izuku shows her Kota's letter, a sign that he is truly on the path to being a hero, does she change her mind about U.A.. She also makes All Might promise to take care of him, and stay alive for him.
While upgrading his costume, Izuku only adds a few things on it and refuses to modify it even further as he is attached to it. He appreciates that his mother made it for him and he wants to keep it as a reminder that she is supporting him.
During their brief reunion for New Years, Izuku lets her know of his predicament with his powers and, while she is worried, Inko is proud of him for saving Eri.
As of the breakout at Tartarus, Inko is aware of the nature of Izuku's acquired Quirk, One For All.

== Hisashi Midoriya ==
Hisashi is his father but he has not been seen much in either his or his wife's life, as his whereabouts and status are unknown. It is unknown if Izuku had a stable relationship with Hisashi considering that he was absent for most of the time. All that is known about him is that he took a work post abroad and that his Quirk allows him to breathe fire.

== All Might ==
Izuku has always admired All Might and strives to become a hero just like he used to be. In their first meeting, All Might saves Izuku from the Sludge Villain and signs the happy boy's notebook. However, Izuku is very shocked to see All Might's true form and is dismayed when the latter tells him being powerless does not fare well at all for his chances at becoming a hero. Despite this, Izuku earns All Might's respect after he runs toward the slime monster in an attempt to save Katsuki, and a mentor-student relationship blooms between the two when All Might chooses him as his successor. While All Might's training on Izuku is rough, he continues to give it his all in the hopes of achieving his dream. All Might and Izuku grow closer when All Might listens to Izuku's drive to train and do more than he has to become a great hero, with All Might impressed by Izuku's resolve.
All Might is Izuku's confidante, mentor, and friend in and out of school, and they share a mutual respect for each other's heroism ideals. All Might returns Izuku's admiration by telling him that he was formerly Quirkless before he was given One For All. All Might is also impressed by how much Izuku has improved his Quirk after his time with Gran Torino, and deems it is time to tell Izuku of their Quirk's history.
Izuku's admiration and respect for All Might are so great that he avoids attacking All Might during the First Term Final Exam, but when it turns out he has no choice but to attack him with one of Katsuki's Grenadier Bracers, Izuku apologizes in advance.
At first, Izuku is an extreme fanboy who tries to imitate All Might from his style of name and costume design. As he matures, later on, Izuku slowly learns to become his own hero and develop his own style. This change is noticed by All Might, who is proud to see Izuku on his way to becoming his own hero, improving his unique skill and style.
Izuku has an almost father-and-son-like relationship with All Might, treating him as a father figure, demanding his respect and the truth from him during times where All Might would rather preserve Izuku's focus on becoming a hero. After the breakout from Tartarus, All Might has taken to supporting Izuku during the boy's vigilante activities, with Izuku keeping his distance from others to avoid All For One homing in on him through the connection between their Quirks.
Unfortunately, after his battle with Lady Nagant and All For One's message to him that "it's his turn", Izuku keeps his distance from All Might as a result of the overwhelming pressure to keep his family and friends safe, and ultimately leaves him behind to go off on his own against All Might's objections. However, after his classmates snap him back to his senses and bring him back to U.A., Izuku's relationship with All Might reverts back to normal, with Izuku apologizing to All Might for his cold behavior and thanking him for helping him as much as he could.

== Recovery Girl ==
Recovery Girl is one of the few people who know about Izuku's Quirk. She often scolds Izuku for using his Quirk dangerously, as he consistently requires the use of her healing Quirk.
After the U.A. Sports Festival, Recovery Girl reaches her breaking point. Fearing that he will continue to cause serious harm to himself as long as he thinks he can just be healed by her, Recovery Girl refuses to heal Izuku's self-inflicted Quirk injuries.

== Shota Aizawa ==
Shota is Izuku's homeroom teacher. Initially, Izuku does not recognize him as a pro-hero and was put off by his deadpan manner. This lasted until the latter uses his Erasure Quirk to restrain him, which causes Izuku to remember he is Eraser Head, a pro-hero that he respects for his power and capabilities. At first, Shota firmly does not believe Izuku can be a hero due to his reckless behavior when using his Quirk, as he would be useless in battle if he kept getting injured. However, Izuku proves to Shota that he has potential; the latter is extremely impressed and sees Izuku's potential in becoming a pro-hero.
Throughout the series, their relationship can be defined as a rocky, troublesome student-teacher relationship. As his teacher, Izuku listens to Shota's orders and advice in order to improve himself. He has some level of respect towards Shota, as he is surprised at the latter handling several villains by himself. However, Shota's cold and logical personality conflicts with Izuku's own, making it difficult for the two to get along. Out of all his students, the exception being Katsuki, Shota appears to antagonize Izuku the most whenever he does something that upsets him, a recurring problem being Izuku overusing his Quirk and breaking his body. As such, Shota refers to Izuku as the 'Problem Child'.
However, Shota is still Izuku's teacher and as such, ultimately cares for him. He offers some level of consolation and guidance for Izuku when he is upset, and he will make sure Izuku does not do anything dangerous and act upon it. In return, Izuku once tries to help Shota when he decides to fight off several members of the League of Villains and is amazed by his abilities since he realizes he greatly underestimated his teacher. However, he is horrified to watch him being overpowered by Nomu at the Unforeseen Simulation Joint. This shows the more caring and inspirational side of Shota helps Izuku get along better with him. Izuku also carries his teacher to safety after All Might appears.
It is possible another reason they sometimes do not get along well is because of Izuku's personality and mindset bearing similarity to All Might's, who Izuku looks up to. Shota and All Might do not get along, and this is extremely evident in Shota and Izuku's relationship.
When Izuku appears to him injured after his battle with Muscular, Shota immediately wants to scold him for being reckless but sees that he only got wounded to protect Kota. He also scolds Izuku for joining in the rescue of Katsuki, which put him in danger.
Regardless, Shota encourages him in the mission to rescue Eri, something that causes Izuku to see that he wants him to atone for betraying his trust.
Recently, Shota has taken note of Izuku's Quirk gradually changing and after asking him about the phenomenon, Shota gets a vague answer.
During the Paranormal Liberation War, when Tomura targets Eraser Head to stop his Erasure Quirk, Deku stops Tomura alongside Katsuki. While holding him back, Deku exclaims that contrary to what Gran Torino said that losing One For All would be the worst-case scenario, losing his teacher who had always looked out for him would be worse, hence Izuku refusing to stand back while Shota's life is on the line; these words touch Eraser Head, and make him more determined to stop Tomura Shigaraki.
During the Final War, just after Deku loses his arms, Shota arrives through Kurogiri's warp gate, using Eri's horn to help return his arms, Shota tells him that he cannot die until he has heard Eri sing. Shota trusts Deku to land the final blow against All For One, telling all the other heroes to clear a path for the young hero.
Eight years after the Final War, Izuku has become a teacher at U.A alongside Shota. Shota however tells him to not be so easy on his students and to be a bit more strict.

== Class 1-A ==
Izuku has formed positive relationships with all of Class 1-A due to his kindness, bravery, and intelligence. He cares about them all dearly, and vice-versa. While most of the class was initially weary of Izuku due to his self-injuring Quirk and mild demeanor, they all quickly came to admire him after seeing his performance in the Battle Trial. Even those who do not initially take kindly to him, most notably Shoto Todoroki, ultimately come to value Izuku for his natural heroics. Initially overwhelmed by how his classmates view him, Izuku quickly came to value his friendships with them, especially as they were his first real friendships since his falling out with Katsuki. It has been shown that part of Katsuki's anger and jealousy toward Izuku is over how popular he is with the rest of the class, in direct contrast with their social standing in middle school.
Izuku's classmates clearly think highly of him and have come to see Izuku as a leader figure, despite his initial shyness and the difficulties he had with his Quirk. When he was initially elected class representative (albeit due to his two first friends - Ochaco and Tenya - casting votes along with him, rather than his actual standing in the class at that point), the class was immediately receptive of him in the role as well as his decision to pass the position over to Tenya. During the Provisional Hero License Exam, his emerging leadership skills play a key role in his class' victory against the other schools. This also shows that almost all of his classmates are willing to listen and follow his lead, and even those he only knows casually respect him enough to follow him. During the events of Mission: Humarise, when Izuku was falsely placed as a suspect on the run for mass murder, all of his classmates refused to believe that he could have done something so terrible.
Following the Paranormal Liberation War, Izuku put enough trust in his classmates to reveal the truth about his Quirk and the reason he's been targeted by Tomura and All For One via personalized letters sent to all of his classmates, before leaving U.A. High to protect them from potential attacks. While he is fighting hired villains, he thinks about all of his classmates and hopes to smile with all of them once he's done. However, Izuku is displeased when Class 1-A - none of whom ever accepted Izuku's departure - come to save him, exposing themselves to the danger he tried to protect them from. So, he tells them to get out of his way, sparking a conflict between him and his friends. During the fight, Class 1-A reminds Izuku of all the times that he had inspired and cared for them, showing how much they really care about him. Later during the Final War, a large segment of the class does everything they can to stand their ground against All For One in a desperate attempt to prevent the villain from reaching Izuku.
After the Final War, when going to where Izuku and Ochaco were talking, they later express sadness at hearing how Deku's quirk is fading. Eight years later, they are all shown to still be in touch, albeit the entire class has rarely been united due to conflicting schedules. It is then shown that all of them, lead by Katsuki, have been funding a special project made by Hatsume and Melissa so Deku could go out and be a hero again, and all of them are then shown charging forward to answer the call.

== Katsuki Bakugo ==
Izuku and Katsuki have known each other since early childhood, but their relationship deteriorated over the years. Ever since he failed to develop a Quirk as a child while Katsuki manifested his Explosion, the latter has seen Izuku as weak and constantly bullied him, giving him the derogatory nickname "Deku", which stems from "Dekunobou" or "someone who can't do anything": a nickname he always refers to him by. In the dub, however, the explanation for the nickname is shortening of "Defenseless Izuku". Interestingly enough, Izuku returns the favor by calling Katsuki his childhood nickname, "Kacchan".
While Katsuki attempts to struggle his way out of the Sludge Villain's grasp, Izuku surprises him by rushing to his aid. Much later, Katsuki catches up with him and berates him for his actions, calling him a Quirkless failure, most likely out of pride. Izuku's heroic act is also what causes All Might to choose him as his successor of One For All, thus making Katsuki indirectly responsible for his acceptance into U.A.
Izuku stands up to Katsuki and enrolls in U.A.. He also reveals his new Quirk, with Katsuki reacting in surprise as the latter angrily questions if Izuku intentionally hid his powers from him. In a training battle, Katsuki attacks Izuku, but once again Izuku surprises Katsuki by revealing he has been memorizing his moves and knows how to counter them, as he once possesses a strategy book that details all of Katsuki and other's moves before Katsuki burns it. Finally standing his ground against Katsuki, Izuku tells him that he is no longer the same kid Katsuki used to bully, even stating he will use the nickname Katsuki gave him as inspiration. Enraged by Izuku's bold claims, Katsuki attacks relentlessly to get him to use his Quirk, which works but ends with Izuku defeating him for once. After the exercise, Izuku catches up to Katsuki and tells him he obtained his powers from someone else, but Katsuki seemingly does not believe it and swears to defeat him.
Izuku states that Katsuki makes all of Izuku's insecurities about himself bubble to the surface. Despite their strained relationship, Izuku still admires him for his strong Quirk, natural combat capabilities, confidence, and his ability to win. Since the end of the Battle Trial Arc, Izuku has not spoken to Katsuki on friendly terms due to Katsuki considering Izuku his arch-rival. Izuku is also surprised to see Katsuki is the one being teased by their class, a complete reversal of their middle school days. Even still, Izuku maintains respect for Katsuki and is shown to care for him, as shown when he tried to ensure Katsuki's safety from the villain attack. Despite Izuku's best efforts, Katsuki is still captured by the Vanguard Action Squad, so Izuku uses all his strength in an attempt to save him and spirals into despair after he fails to do so. Directly after, Izuku teams up with Tenya, Eijiro, Shoto, and Momo in an attempt to rescue Katsuki from the League of Villains' hideout.
Back at U.A. sometime later, Katsuki remarks to Izuku that he is really making his borrowed power his, revealing he believes Izuku's story of obtaining a Quirk, which worries Izuku.
Later on, Katsuki and Izuku take out their stress on each other in an emotional fight, which Katsuki wins. After the fight, Katsuki learns the full truth of Izuku's Quirk and his connection to All Might, which he seems to understand and decides to keep the secret to himself.
Unbeknownst to him or Katsuki, Shota has seen their bond having a big effect on the rest of the Class 1-A despite that they are not leaders or the most popular. Izuku and Katsuki's passion inspire everyone in the class and raises the standard for success.
Since then, the two better understand each other and have become more reminiscent of that of a proper "friendly" rivalry, although they still have a long way to go. Katsuki is willing to work with Izuku and be less malicious towards him. He willingly fought alongside Izuku who temporarily gave him One for All. When Izuku temporarily gave it to him, they thought that the action would end Izuku's dream and that he was going to be Quirkless again. Together, they were able to defeat Nine. In the end, both of them talked to each other and were smiling, showing that their fierce rivalry gradually becoming more friendly.
The two have shown to be at a recovery period in their relationship. They're allies in battle, and support as well as challenge each others' strengths in weaknesses. Their core fundamentals being that Katsuki wants to win while Izuku wants to save. All Might encourages both of their philosophies as well as their mending friendship. Katsuki has yet to apologize, and both him and Izuku have yet to reach a comfortable stage of their friendship where they refer to each other as friends. Their dynamic has seen the most growth and significance in the series, having a movie centering around their team-up, and dynamic duo titles such as "the Wonder Duo", and "Twin Stars".
In the Paranormal Liberation War Arc, Katsuki went along with Izuku and protected him, showing that Katsuki does care for him. Although he doesn't admit it, he does show some level of respect for him and considers him his true rival for the number one hero spot. When Deku's life was in peril as Tomura launched Rivet Stab at him, Katsuki rises up and is injured by the attack. Deku, already enraged up from all the wrongs Tomura had committed, turns to a feral state.
Later on, during the Dark Hero Arc, Izuku starts to become exhausted from all the stress and burden. Dictator uses his Quirk to send a mob of citizens to attack Izuku. However, Katsuki actively saves Izuku from Dictator using AP Shot to defeat him and freeing all the citizens.
After Tenya successfully retrieves Izuku, Katsuki confesses that his body moved on it's own to save Izuku, the true nature of his bullying before calling him by his real name and apologizing for his prior bullying.
Even after moving back to the Height's Alliance, their relationship has hardly change when it comes to becoming the no. 1 Hero. Izuku and Katsuki continues to have a stable respect for one another, and in the beginning of the Final War, Izuku and Katsuki continues to work together for the final battle against All For One and Tomura. However, Izuku was caught in in a trap by Himiko which dragged him to a different portal created by Monoma.
After Izuku escaped from Okuto Island, he was generally worried about Katsuki and the other classmates until he returns to the U.A Flying Sky Fortress. Upon arriving, he was devastated when he found Katsuki's corpse, having to be killed by Tomura, causing him to go into shock. Seeing all the other barely conscious Heroes sends Izuku into a further induced stress, while "Tomura" mocks him for his tardiness. With his fear realized about Katsuki's death, his stress turned into rage which once again overwhelm Izuku as his Quirks begin to spiral out of control until Mirio Togata reappears to Izuku. He attempts to talk some sense into him, telling him that everyone is still breathing and that Edgeshot is saving Katsuki's life, so they need to keep on fighting because they haven't lost anyone yet.
It was right then that Izuku calms his nerves and entrusts his hopes to Edgeshot to revive Katsuki while he engages Tomura in a fated battle.
Later, once All For One has All Might at his mercy and no one is left to save him, Katsuki emerges from the floating U.A. fortress, to Izuku's astonishment. He immediately rockets forward and seamlessly grabs Izuku's hand so he can receive an additional boost to save their childhood hero. Once All Might is saved, Izuku entrusts Katsuki with stopping All For One, while he focuses on defeating Tomura.
Katsuki joins Izuku once again during the final battle against "All For One" when he blasts Kurogiri in order to prevent him from getting pulled into another Warp Gate. Once the war is over, Katsuki worries about Izuku once again. Upon reaching his and All Might's room, he overhears their conversation about how Izuku still has the embers of One For All and that he would eventually end up Quirkless. This revelation brings Katsuki to tears as he laments if he'll still be able to compete with him once again, only to be reassured by the latter.
Once Izuku returns to being Quirkless again, Katsuki and his fellow classmates would eventually fund the creation of a mechanical suit using the data gathered from All Might's battle with All For One 8 years ago. The suit was created with the help of Mei and Melissa, and it was eventually delivered to Izuku by All Might himself. Once he received the suit, Katsuki then calls out to his friend to join him in their Hero work. About a month later, Katsuki subtly tries to invite Izuku to work at his agency, but is turned down. He also tells Izuku to think more highly of himself, warning him that giving everyone special treatment means that no one is truly special to him, words that later prompt Izuku to reconnect with Ochaco and confess his feelings for her.
After Izuku's return to hero work, he and Katsuki move up in the hero rankings to No. 4 and 5 respectively, continuing to compete for the rest of their life, just how they had promised.

== Ochaco Uraraka ==
Not counting Katsuki and All Might whom he knew beforehand, Ochaco is the first person that Izuku interacted with at U.A. High School, wherein prior to the entrance exam, she used her Quirk to stop him from falling on the ground after he tripped over his own foot. In turn, he saved her from being attacked by a giant robot and she returned the favor by ensuring he landed safely on the ground after jumping to her rescue, afterwards trying to appeal to Present Mic to give him her own exam points so he would pass out of further gratitude. The two met again after becoming classmates and soon became close best friends alongside Tenya Ida; they get along very well and their discussions are always bright and friendly. They share similar ways of quick thinking, tenacity, perseverance, and high spirit, which contributes to their close friendship.
From the beginning, it is indicated that Izuku has a crush on Ochaco even before she herself developed a crush on him, but lacks the self-confidence to realize or act on it. In their first meeting, Izuku was too stunned to speak, before afterwards congratulating himself on talking to a girl (even though he didn't actually say anything). A running gag is Izuku blushing and getting flustered whenever Ochaco happens gets too physically close to him, though this occasionally translates to other girls. Ochaco is the one who turned his once demeaning nickname "Deku" into a positive identity for him, saying it gave her a "never gives up" vibe. Although Izuku seemed to initially accept the name solely because Ochaco liked it, he came to value her meaning of it during their battle with Katsuki. This led to him choosing it as his hero name and acknowledging Ochaco as the motivation for it, which touched Ochaco just as much as it irritated Katsuki.
They mutually respect each other, as Ochaco is inspired by Izuku, striving for his level of tenacity and perseverance. They are protective of one another, as Ochaco quickly called the police after she found Tomura threatening Izuku. Izuku thinks of her as a brave and resourceful person by herself and considers her very capable of devising strategic plans on her own. He even defends her from Katsuki when he thought she was incapable of fighting on her own and had help from Izuku.
Another example of his confidence in Ochaco is seen during the Provisional Hero License Exam. Himiko Toga (disguised as Camie Utsushimi) used her Quirk to impersonate Ochaco, but Izuku saw right through her disguise, as "Ochaco" jumped into danger without a plan, something he knew that the real Ochaco would never do. When Himiko sees Ochaco, she tells her that Izuku trusts her immensely.
When they first met, Izuku was barely able to hold a conversation with her, due to his timidity towards girls, but as he gets to know her more he is able to speak confidently with her, but still sometimes blushes when she stands too close to him.
Over time, Ochaco has developed a crush on Izuku, and in turn Izuku's feelings seem to grow. For example, he felt immensely touched when Ochaco turned his nickname "Deku" into a positive identity for him, that inspired him to choose it as his official hero name. He also confidently defended Ochaco from Katsuki when the latter thought she fought him valiantly only because she had guidance from Izuku. Other small interactions like being flustered when caught by Ochaco touring I-Island with Melissa Shield further supports this, along with growing tenderly concerned for her during their battle against Nine on Nabu Island. It’s also shown that while Izuku is very easily flustered when any girl comes close to him, only Ochaco can make his whole face go red, cluing in that he likes her more than other girls.
While participating in a joint training exercise with 1-B, when Izuku was first manifesting Blackwhip, Ochaco was the first to realize he couldn't control it and came to his aid. After, she showed was concerned for his well-being. His reckless regard for himself had lead to her understanding heroes need protecting too, and she makes a vow to watch out for anybody who needs it as a hero who saves people to help them and not just for money. He would also inspire her to add hooks to her gauntlets for rescue a while later.
Afterward, at Christmas, she and Izuku receive each other's Secret Santa gifts with ecstatic looks on their faces. Ochaco got Izuku a bag of Mochi Chips, and Izuku got her an All Might keychain. Ochaco has taken to carrying this keychain he gave her on her belt, causing Mina to tease her. But at that time, Ochaco smiled and responded in a slightly nervous way while putting the keychain away, indicating that she felt a little less embarrassed about her feelings for Izuku. After starting up work studies again, Class 1-A showed off their new moves to All Might. Ochaco and Izuku shared a touching moment where they thank each other for making one another stronger, fist-bumping. This is witnessed by the rest of the class, cluing them in to their budding relationship.
During the Paranormal Liberation War, when Izuku broke off from the group and decided to go off on his own alongside Katsuki, Ochaco watches him go off while feeling concerned about him. After the battle, when Izuku fell into a coma, Kyoka asked if Izuku was alright, in which Ochaco responded that she feels worried as she stood in front of the door of his hospital room.
While Izuku continued conversing with the predecessors while unconscious, All Might left the room, and Ochaco was the first person to go in to check on him. Back at Heights Alliance in April, Ochaco and her classmates received individual letters from Izuku. Her letter said how Izuku thanks her for everything, confessing the truth behind his Quirk: that he received it from All Might, how All For One and Tomura are after him, and that he must leave because of it. After she read her letter, she calls him a dummy for his rash decision making, deeply worried.
She later joins the rest of 1-A in tracking him down to bring him back to U.A. When it's Ochaco's turn to speak with him, she explains she and the others are more than willing to go after Izuku even though she understands he's only trying to keep them safe. Before Ochaco can finish her sentence, Izuku tries to run away. Ochaco gathered her classmates to launch Ida after Izuku to capture him, thinking to herself how she knows that she and everyone has something to say to him but they know their words alone won't have any effect. She acknowledges the class don't want to be protected by Izuku, or that they're rejecting Izuku's own beliefs and his actions. She and the others just want to be with him.
When the class succeed in rescuing Izuku, they bring him back to school. Yet as he suspects, the civilians refuse to let him inside. They know he's the rumored boy Tomura was pursuing. After they were convinced U.A. would provide them sanctuary, they're scared about allowing in what they consider a "ticking time-bomb". Izuku is about to leave but Ochaco grabs his hand to stop him and tells him it's okay, as she is determined to not let him go.
She shouts down the crowd on behalf of Izuku and everybody's sakes, firmly rebutting every criticism thrown against him and the heroes with compassion and logic. Izuku, deeply moved and thankful for her passionate defense of him, is reduced to tears and falls to his knees. Thanks to her speech, Izuku is allowed inside and is able to rest properly for the first time in weeks.
After shacking up at the dorms, Izuku asks where Ochaco is and wants to thank her personally, but she already went to sleep because the day's events had exhausted her. Then Cathleen Bate buys the heroes of Japan time to regroup and track down the villains with her sacrifice against Tomura and All For One. And then the reveal of Aoyama being a spy for All For One also puts a great strain on the class dynamic as they zero in on destroying the diabolical mastermind for good, making things far too busy as everybody prepares for a second war.
Ultimately, it would take another week of waiting for Izuku to finally get the chance to speak to Ochaco personally at Class 1-A's new mobile stronghold, Troy. He meets her on the cliffs overlooking the damaged cityscape the day before the operation against All For One, thanking her profusely for her speech. This makes Ochaco embarrassed as he keeps showering her in compliments, and when she calls herself weird Izuku rambles about how pretty her hair looks making her further flustered. They then get to discussing Himiko, with Ochaco relating that she held complicated feelings toward the serial killer, but realized it wasn't fair to judge her too harshly since she knew nothing about her. She makes it clear she can never forgive her, but she's thinking about reaching out and attempting to save her all the same.
Izuku is pleasantly surprised to hear this, as he himself holds similar feelings toward Tomura. He explains he felt the presence of a little boy inside him and wants to save him if possible. Ochaco chuckles and remarks "both of us are kind of weird". They spend some more time together in silence, surveying the damaged city as they wait out the final hours before their upcoming confrontation with All For One and the rest of the League, knowing it'll be the fight of their lives.
Later, during his final confrontation against Tomura, despite the villain mocking at his attempts to "save him", Izuku stands his ground, using his memories of Ochaco as inspiration to keep fighting.
After Izuku finally defeated All For One once and for all, he attempted to talk to Ochaco about something important, but was unable to as she quickly deflected the conversation. Izuku however, would go on to express deep concern for Ochaco, noticing how she was putting on a cheerful front to mask her pain and grief over the death of Himiko. Finding her alone and in anguish one night on the hillside outside of Troy, Izuku insists that Ochaco let out all of her repressed feelings, taking note of how she always prioritized others over herself. Recalling how she has always saved him every step of the way on his journey, Izuku openly calls Ochaco his hero and takes her hand to comfort her, finally allowing her to cry her heart out over her failure to save Himiko. Being able to relate to her feelings as he felt similarly over his failure to save Tomura, Izuku assures Ochaco that so long as they continue to go above and beyond to reach out to others even when nobody's asking, their actions will continue to make a difference.
Eight years after the Final War ended, the former Class 1-A students get together for a party to congratulate Shoto on reaching the No. 2 spot in the Hero Rankings. By this point, both Izuku and Ochaco are being pushed to think more about their personal lives and wants by those close to them (Tsuyu for Ochaco and Katsuki for Izuku) due to their selfless personalities. During said dinner, Izuku watches her smiling and laughing with her friends, thinking back to Katsuki's words about giving "special treatment" to others. After stopping a car heist together and going their separate ways, Izuku realizes his feelings for Ochaco and goes looking for her, finding her alone in a crowd. He tells her that, while he respects and loves everyone, he really wants to spend more time with her now, even into the future. They both blush, as Ochaco is pushed up close to Izuku by Himiko's spirit, telling her to embrace her own feelings and live her life the way she wants to. Ochaco happily replies that Izuku's feelings were mutual, much to Izuku's joy, and the two clasp their hands together to start a new relationship.

== Tenya Ida ==
In their first meeting, Tenya labeled Izuku's muttering about heroes as a distraction and thought he would be a hindrance to the school which earned Izuku bad attention from his peers. However, in the entrance tests, Izuku surprised Tenya by selflessly going back to save Ochaco and using his Quirk to destroy a giant robot in the process, and was the only one to deduce that Izuku's motivations were entirely altruistic.
When they met again in class, Izuku first thought that Tenya was scary akin to Katsuki and that they wouldn't get along, but was surprised when Tenya instead immediately apologized for his previous treatment to him and (mistakenly believing that Izuku had figured out the Rescue Points system of testing on his own) even said he considered Izuku better than him in many ways after the entrance course, much to Izuku's bafflement. Later that day, Tenya offered to walk home with Izuku, and they were joined by Ochaco. Subsequently, Izuku got to know Tenya better, where he found out that while the latter was serious he was also kind and they ended up becoming best friends alongside Ochaco.
Izuku and Tenya are usually friendly with each other. They are usually seen together having friendly discussions. Izuku resigned as Class President and named Tenya as his successor, knowing that he has better leadership qualities than him. After Tenya is defeated by Shoto, Izuku is saddened to see his friend lose. Tenya has often compared Izuku to his brother, for whom he has great admiration for.
However, sometimes their friendship slightly falters such as when Izuku criticized Tenya for seeking revenge against Stain and Tenya punching Izuku in the face for acting recklessly by attempting to save Katsuki. Despite this, Izuku and Tenya's friendship has rebounded back many times despite these mishaps, mainly because they are only concerned for each other's well being.
In the Stain situation, Izuku was deeply concerned about Tenya's mental state and tried to console him, but Tenya brushed this off and affirmed their friendship. Not buying this, Izuku tries to save him from his death and would later do so alongside Shoto Todoroki. While recuperating at Hosu General Hospital, both Izuku and Tenya resolve to become stronger together. After this particular encounter, Tenya admires Izuku even more, valuing how he saved Tenya not only from death but also his sinister thoughts, similar to Shoto.
During the climax of the battle when Class 1-A combines their powers to catch up to Izuku, Tenya is chosen to be the one to grab Izuku, due to his leadership role, close friendship with Izuku, and being the speedster of the class. Through the combined efforts of the class, Tenya gets launched into the air and grabs Izuku's hand. Tenya would say that Ingenium is the Hero who will grab the hand of a lost child and that giving help that's not asked for, is what makes a true Hero, paralleling Izuku's words to Tenya that meddling where you don't need to is the essence of being a Hero. These words would cause Izuku to openly break into tears upon hearing those words, and (along with Katsuki's apology and Ochaco's speech later on) would be the catalyst to him conceding.

== Shoto Todoroki ==
Shoto originally saw Izuku as his arch-rival, due to him having connections with All Might (a person with whom his father has a major rivalry with). Izuku, on the other hand, has always held great respect for Shoto, admitting that he is far stronger than him. Shoto confronts Izuku at the U.A. Sports Festival, asking if he is All Might's secret love child, but the latter denies this as Shoto decides to reveal his family history. After hearing Shoto's tragic backstory and seeing his sadness, Izuku starts to show sympathy for the pain he went through and resolves to help him overcome his dark past by getting him to use his power, telling Shoto that the power is his own and not his father's. Although Izuku loses to Shoto while trying to help him, Izuku's actions do have an impact as Shoto starts reacquainting himself with parts of his past he had forgotten and stops rejecting his power. Shoto also recognizes that Izuku knowingly insisted on helping him, despite knowing it would lead to Shoto's victory.
Shoto even comes to his aid when Izuku and Tenya are attacked by Stain, telling his father he was going to "help a friend" when he received Izuku's location. They work well together, going on to defeat the infamous hero killer. After the battle, Izuku and Shoto are on friendly terms with each other, with Izuku even laughing at Shoto's expense when he was worried that he was cursed to always hurt people's hands. They are good friends thereafter — Shoto, who was previously shown to have little interactions with his classmates, starts to hang around with Izuku and his friends more frequently, even joining their lunch table. Throughout the series, Izuku has had subtle parallels with Shoto's mother, Rei Todoroki. From giving Shoto eerily similar glances, Rei during her mental breakdown, and Izuku during his match with Shoto, to Shoto being openly vulnerable around both of them. Shoto has a deep love for his mother, and a deep friendship with Izuku as he was the one who inspired him to reach out to others.
Izuku is shown to worry if Shoto learns of his power, a good example was seen when Shoto asks him of his second Quirk when it awakes in his match but Izuku is able to cast it aside.
Shoto offers both Izuku and Katsuki a chance to intern with him and his father signifying the bond he's made with the two. During the Endeavor Internship arc, he invites the two over for dinner where they meet his family and learn more about the history of the Todoroki's. Tensions grow when Natsuo refuses to accept the normalcy of seeing his father take on an attentive role, believing he still hasn't taken accountability. Izuku intervenes to help Shoto explain his feelings. He claims that he believes that Shoto is preparing himself to forgive his father, and that he's a "caring person". It's heavily implied that he assesses this through his internalized feelings towards Katsuki and their rough past. Natsuo confirms Izuku's way of describing Shoto, explaining that he couldn't do the same because he's not as caring as Shoto. Just before that, Izuku called Shoto by his hero name, which also is his given name "Shoto". Upon departing their internship, Shoto's sister, Fuyumi, shares Izuku's hand and thanks him for being Shoto's friend.
During Shoto's fight with his older brother in the Paranormal Liberation War, despite having all of his limbs broken, Deku fires his Blackwhip from his mouth to assist Shoto. When Dabi tells Deku to stay out of the fight since it was none of his business, Deku replies that it was his business because he is Shoto's friend. Later on, Shoto would openly criticize Endeavor for letting Deku and All Might work alone, and during the battle between Class 1-A and Deku, tells Deku to let them share his burden of defeating All For One. He later joined the rest of the class in tracking him down to bring him back. He prevented his escape and told him to let the class join him in taking on his burden, knowing it was taking an emotional toll on him.

Tsuyu even notices the similarities between Izuku and All Might's powers, which worried him so he has to give her a vague answer.

== Yuga Aoyama ==

However, Izuku surprises him with his powerful Quirk which allows him to remain in the school.

Izuku is later scared when Yuga spies on him as he sleeps and leaves a message (in cheese) to say he knows Izuku's secret, which left Izuku wondering if Yuga is aware of the truth about his Quirk. In training, Izuku is called out to by Yuga and is shown one of his new moves, though it causes Yuga severe stomach problems to use. Seeing him in this state, Izuku takes Yuga away to recuperate before he asks him what he meant in his message.

After a few days, Izuku and Yuga become good friends, with some of the class noting on their more frequent interactions.
During the U.A. School Festival, Izuku and Yuga make a frayed rope, with the latter claiming it as a symbol of their friendship. He is tasked with securing Yuga as the disco ball since the class knew of their bond.
After the war, he was stunned to discover from Toru that Yuga was the traitor who had been aiding All For One. When confronting him, Izuku revealed that he noticed Yuga was not being his usual self while Yuga admitted with great remorse that this was all true. Yuga also noted how he fell into despair upon learning of their similar stories on being Quirkless, before he reluctantly tried to attack only for Toru to intervene. Izuku managed to stop him and his parents from fleeing and brings them back to the school grounds for interrogation. During so, Izuku tells Yuga that despite learning that he was the traitor, he doesn't bear any ill will to him, comparing him to Lady Nagant; a person who's spirit was crushed, but not their soul, and citing all the other good things he has done over the school year. He reaches his hand out to him and tells him that he still believes he can be a hero, and that no one should be labeled a villain for the rest of their lives for committing a single crime.

== Kota Izumi ==
At first, Kota despises Izuku and Class 1-A for always working so hard to better themselves against fighting villains. He even goes as far as to hit Izuku in the crotch for trying to be friendly towards him, as he voices his dislike of heroes. In spite of this, Izuku holds no grudge and tries to get along with Kota due to their similar circumstances of both being "Quirkless" (Izuku not originally being born with a Quirk and Kota refusing to use his Quirk), though he has no luck due to a disagreement in their ideals. Because of his past, Kota ignores anything that dealt with heroes and villains, and even Quirks altogether. After finding his secret hideout, Izuku tries talking to Kota, only for him to snap at Izuku because he's never gone through what Kota has.
However, Izuku saves Kota from Muscular (who is also the same villain that killed Kota's parents) after realizing he is in danger. When Kota asks why Izuku took it upon himself to save him, especially by how he treated him, Izuku states he had seen the latter was in danger and he had to save him. Izuku shields Kota from the Muscle Augmentation villain at all costs, even going past his limitations just to keep Kota safe.
During the battle, Kota uses his Quirk out of desperation in an attempt to help Izuku. From this, it is shown Kota has gained respect for Izuku and Kota would watch Izuku overpower and defeat Muscular. After the battle, he tells Kota that his parents died to protect him and says he risked his life for him because he wants his acknowledgment as a hero. Izuku then tells Kota to help save others just as he helped save him.
At the hospital, Izuku soon gets a letter from Kota, who apologizes for his previous treatment towards him and thanks him for saving his life. He is extremely happy to have impacted on someone, especially someone who had originally hated heroes. Izuku later uses the letter to show All Might and his mother that he is well on his way of being a hero after having an effect on someone.
As Izuku reunites with Kota, when the Wild, Wild Pussycats visited U.A., and Izuku is happy to see him and thanks him for the letter and tells Kota that he'll treasure it forever. Shino draws his attention to Kota's new red high-top sneakers, which closely resemble Izuku's own signature shoes, to Kota's embarrassment. Then, Shino reveals that Kota insisted on having red shoes—an indication of the admiration and connection between them. Despite Kota's reluctance to admit it, Izuku sees that Kota now looks up to him. When Izuku happily points out their matching shoes, Kota can't help but smile for the first time. demonstrating the development of their bond.
Kota has left a lasting impression on Izuku, broadening his understanding of the world and challenging his assumptions about hero society. During the training camp arc, upon finding out about Kota's resentment towards quirks and hero society, Izuku recalled his encounter with Tomura, whose hatred towards hero society initially seemed unfathomable to Izuku. This realization led Izuku to acknowledge the flaws and failures of the system that had shaped both Kota's and Tomura's perspectives, and to recognize that the hero society he had always idolized was not perfect.
Later, during Izuku's second encounter with Muscular, his futile attempt at empathizing with the villain leads him to further reflect on Kota's transformation from an angry, resentful child to a kind and happy boy. This memory inspires hope within Izuku that perhaps, even villains like Tomura could find redemption and a path away from violence and destruction. In this moment of contemplation, Izuku can't help but wonder if, had he understood this possibility sooner, things could have turned out differently—a realization that fuels his determination to continue seeking understanding and empathy in a world filled with conflict and violence. The lessons learned from his relationship with Kota thus drive Izuku's continued belief in the possibility of a better, more understanding world, where even villains can be reached through compassion and empathy.
After months of fighting alone to protect his loved ones, Izuku faces resistance upon his forced return to U.A. by civilians who reject him. However, Ochako's heartfelt speech in support of Izuku's heroism deeply moves him. Overwhelmed by the tumultuous situation and Ochako's words, Izuku falls to his knees. Inspired by her speech, Kota runs to comfort Izuku, calling out "Midoriya-niichan!" As Kota embraces him, he apologizes for his fear and confesses that he wants to be like Izuku—a brave and selfless hero. Moved by Kota's admission, Izuku recognizes the impact he has had on Kota's life and the deep bond they share, feeling a renewed sense of determination and gratitude for their friendship.
As Class 1-A readies for the Final War, a visibly worried Kota approaches Izuku, hesitantly asking if he's truly leaving U.A. Izuku confirms his departure, and the emotional weight of the moment is palpable. When it's time for farewells, Izuku's bond with Kota is solidified as he shares this tender moment not only with his mother but also included Kota, further emphasizing the depth of their connection and brotherly bond, and the unique place Kota holds in Izuku's life.
As the final war unfolds, Izuku's positive influence on Kota's heart and heroic spirit becomes evident when Kota demonstrates concern for Izuku's well-being during his intense battle with Tomura Shigaraki. Drawing inspiration from Izuku, Kota comforts a distraught Eri by holding her hand, showcasing how even small acts of compassion can be heroic—a testament to the depth of their connection. Moreover, Kota embraces the "crybaby" trait he shares with Izuku, asserting that his emotional vulnerability only strengthens his resolve in adversity. This heartfelt acknowledgment of their shared qualities emphasizes the profound impact Izuku has had on Kota, as he finds inspiration in the very trait that Izuku has struggled with, demonstrating the resilience and determination they both embody as heroes. Their mutual growth and emotional connection illustrate just how deep their bond has become over time.
During the Final War, Izuku's connection with Kota deepens as Kota is rooting for Izuku during his climactic battle against All For One. Kota's support for Izuku is evident as he calls out to him using the affectionate nickname "Midoriya-niichan" (Big brother-Midoriya), reflecting their strong brotherly bond. This event highlights the mutual growth and emotional connection in their relationship, as well as the impact of Kota's unwavering belief in Izuku during a critical moment. As Izuku prepares to engage in battle, Kota can be seen shouting 'Do your best!' offering a heartfelt encouragement to Izuku during this crucial moment.
Izuku's influence on Kota was profound, inspiring him to follow in his footsteps and enroll in UA's hero course. Through their interactions, Izuku's heroic example and caring nature encouraged Kota to embrace the path of heroism, despite his initial distrust of heroes. Despite the passage of time and changes in their circumstances, Kota's relationship with Izuku remains deeply personal. Even in formal settings where he is expected to address Izuku as 'Deku-sensei,' Kota sometimes accidentally calls him 'Midoriya-niichan.' This slip-up illustrates how deeply ingrained the affectionate address is, showcasing the enduring nature of their close brotherly bond, persisting despite the changing dynamics of their lives.
Kota's hero costume reflects his deep admiration, respect and love for Izuku. Kota’s hero costume, while similar to his parents’, notably features a green vest in place of their red ones. The change in color from red to green may indicate a deliberate choice on Kota's part to honor Izuku, as an important part of his life and hero journey as green is a color closely associated to Izuku, and whose costume is predominantly green.
Kota and Izuku's relationship has experienced a gradual and profound transformation throughout their interactions, which can be observed in the way Kota addresses Izuku. Initially, Kota referred to Izuku as 'temee,' a derogatory term, during the training camp arc. However, Izuku's selfless act of saving Kota from Muscular marked a turning point in their relationship, prompting Kota to address Izuku as 'Midoriya-san' in his thank-you letter, demonstrating growing respect and appreciation. Most recently, Kota has begun calling Izuku 'Midoriya-niichan,' meaning 'big brother Midoriya,' indicating his deep affection, admiration, and the closeness of their bond. This evolution in Kota's addresses underscores the profound impact Izuku has had on his life, evolving from animosity to a brotherly bond founded on mutual respect and admiration between the two of them. Years later, this affectionate address has solidified into a habit, with Kota still calling Izuku 'Midoriya-niichan,' even in formal settings where he is expected to address him as 'Deku-sensei.' This demonstrates the enduring nature of their brotherly bond, persisting even as Kota matures and their relationship takes on different dimensions, such as student and teacher.

== Tomura Shigaraki ==
Tomura is Izuku's arch-nemesis. Due to them being groomed to be the successors of their respective mentors, Izuku and Tomura mutually despise each other; Tomura despises Izuku for his admiration of All Might (someone he hates) while Izuku is disgusted with Tomura's villainous actions. During the U.S.J. Arc, Izuku saved All Might, which caused Tomura to take an interest in Izuku, as shown when he watched Izuku's match during U.A.'s Sports Festival.
Later in Kiyashi Ward Shopping Mall, they unintentionally met each other again, so Tomura decided to talk with Izuku about the difference between him and Stain. After hearing Izuku's thoughts on the matter and before leaving the shopping mall, Tomura declared that the next time they meet, he would show Izuku no mercy.
Tomura is considered to be a dark reflection of Izuku. While Izuku works toward becoming the greatest hero, Tomura strives to become the greatest villain. Izuku and Tomura share many similarities, such as a preference for wearing red sneakers and initially having near-total dependence on their respective mentor figures before gradually developing as their own distinct hero and villain. Interestingly, when Tomura was five years old, he bore a striking resemblance to Izuku. As young children, both Tenko and Izuku had a strong admiration for heroes.
Later on, during the Paranormal Liberation War, Deku goes into battle with Tomura when he learns that he intends to come after his Quirk. While originally attempting to avoid him, he engages him along with Katsuki and the other heroes in an attempt to protect Eraser Head. As the fight goes on, Deku's hatred for Tomura continues to grow, and it only worsens when he sees the villain impale Gran Torino in the chest. After seeing this, Deku screams and grabs Tomura, declaring that he will never forgive the villain, bearing the same amount of hatred for Tomura as the latter would never forgive anyone like him because he is a hero.
However, after Tomura's attempt to steal One For All, Izuku witnesses him being usurped by and trying to fight back control against All For One, who eventually succeeds in taking command of his main body. Despite all the terrible, unforgivable things he's done, Izuku still thinks back to Tomura's troubled face and recognizes him as someone who needed saving.
When conversing with the One For All vestiges, they all understand Izuku's hesitation, having sensed his emotions when he first noticed Tomura suffering. Regardless, Nana states that Tomura is likely beyond saving and urges him to find the resolve to kill him if necessary. In response, Izuku reflects on how he felt Tomura's emotions while inside the mental world, and he was able to see a little boy crying inside. Izuku notes that while Tomura did kill many people and hurt many friends, One For All was meant to save people, and ultimately, while he does acknowledge the possibility that he may have to kill Tomura, Izuku still voices his desire to try and save Tomura first.
Once Izuku defeats All For One's vestige within Tomura's body, it allowed him to emerge once again. While his central aspect as "Tenko" was still crying out to Izuku to save him, it could only manifest itself through continued spite in his present self as "Tomura", pushing Izuku away by declaring he was "[his] villain."
Upon finally regaining complete control of his body, Tomura announces that he has no desire for One For All, proving that his previous obsession with stealing the Quirk was merely an extension of his master's will. "Tomura Shigaraki" and "Tenko Shimura" state their one and only hope: the destruction of everything that stemmed from the abusive house he grew up in and the eradication of everything associated with it, telling Izuku it will be the only thing that will save him. The two archenemies clash, with Tomura Intending to destroy Izuku, the only person in the world with the power to stop him, once and for all.
During their final battle, Tomura constantly mocks Izuku's continued desire to "save him" even as the fate of the world hangs in the balance. Tomura states that people interpret the world around them through narrow, limited windows, and when the world throws the inexplicable at them, they concoct rationalities to put themselves at ease, and tells Izuku that he is simply cramming Tomura into his own narrow worldview. He tells Deku that just like he is able to see the One For All vestiges inside him, he is able to see deep inside himself, as within Tomura is a visage of crumbling buildings melded together burying the persona of Tenko Shimura.
In order to defeat him, Izuku begins transferring the vestiges of One For All's wielders, hitting this mental core he's hidden away. Tomura's instinctive fear of confronting the source of his trauma visibly terrifies him like nothing he's dealt with before, screaming at Izuku to stay away from him. As he does so, he flashes back to Kotaro trying to push him away, reminding himself of the way his own father acted in a moment of desperation. By reaching the core, Izuku manages to reach through to Tomura after holding his hand and preventing him from decaying Hana, even at the cost of his arms. Izuku stymies his desire for vengeance through this act of pure, honest altruism, and it prompts him to open up. Tomura explains that even if Izuku could stamp out his hatred, or become "hollow," he'd still need to be the "hero" for the villains who required one.
Unfortunately, Izuku overcoming Tomura's hatred prompts the resurgence of All For One, who emerges from the house Tomura grew up in and explains how he manipulated Kotaro into giving birth to Tenko and gave him the Decay Quirk that caused the deaths of his family, separating the two from their catharsis. He manages to "delete" Tomura with all that remains being a lingering echo, as he bears down on Izuku for seemingly sacrificing Yoichi's vestige along with One For All.
In the end, Tomura joins forces with the One For All users and Izuku to defeat All For One for good after Izuku reawakened them all through resonance with his final One For All ember, punching his and Yoichi's quirk factors together. Tomura admits to Izuku he may have been right; that maybe he was just a crying kid on the inside who couldn't even destroy his hands, which had been rewound back by Eri's horn. Izuku explains that while he couldn't forgive Tomura, he fought because he wanted his suffering to end so it wouldn't spread to other people. Tomura requests Izuku to tell Spinner that he fought for destruction until the end; Izuku, however, says he'd already managed to destroy what he needed to, and the two former enemies part ways on amicable terms. Later, Izuku is shown to feel guilty for failing to save Tenko’s life.
When visiting Spinner, Izuku encouraged Spinner to write a comic about Tomura, promising he would never forget him. 8 years later, as Izuku becomes a hero again, he looks back and sees Tomura’s ghost smiling at him.

High.

== All For One ==
While he and Izuku went a long time without directly interacting, All For One has made it his mission to hunt down every One For All wielder he can find in an attempt to steal the Quirk and make his brother, the original user, become one with him. When Izuku became the ninth and final user of One For All, it makes All For One his most dangerous foe alongside Tomura, as he inherits him as a nemesis by accepting All Might's power.
Once All For One deduced that Izuku was All Might's successor, he prepared to crush the boy's heroic spirit with an all-or-nothing plan to destroy him and acquire One For All at last. During his time in Tartarus, All For One lost interest in All Might and moved on to Izuku, fantasizing about tormenting him. Izuku, on the other hand, knew there was a chance he'd have to go up against One For All's mortal enemy someday even if the details of the supervillain's plan wouldn't be deduced until months after his imprisonment.
Upon witnessing All For One in the flesh, Izuku felt overwhelming fear, seeing visions of him and his friends dead by his hands. Izuku's feelings toward All For One became extremely spiteful near the end of the Paranormal Liberation War, when the latter takes over Tomura's body and stabs Katsuki, following it up with dismissing the efforts of all the heroes trying to stop Tomura as "pointless". Enraged, Izuku lost his calm and went straight toward Tomura/All For One, leaving him vulnerable to getting One For All stolen.
In the vestige world, All For One mocked Izuku's altruism as a weakness and claimed that All Might made a mistake choosing him as a successor. Yoichi and Nana, however, fought back against him, rebutting that Izuku's perceived weakness is exactly why he was chosen for this power. All For One fails in stealing One For All, and while he acknowledges Izuku as a threat to his plans, he is still dismissive of the young hero.
All For One finds Izuku to be predictable, and exploits this quality to try and break Izuku's spirit. He anticipated that Izuku would reach out to Lady Nagant, so he detonated her using the bomb quirk he'd planted into her in secret. He also left a video message at the former Creature Rejection Clan hideout when the heroes investigated it, telling Izuku that society will always exclude certain people and label them villains, so he is fighting an endless and ultimately pointless battle. All For One's manipulation makes Izuku paranoid to the point that he doesn't try to reach out to other villains he sends his way and begins working alone, making civilians fearful of the hero. His attempts to exhaust and isolate Izuku were thwarted when Class 1-A brought him back to U.A., though he shrugged it off.
Izuku's hatred toward All For One would grow further when he learns that he forced Yuga Aoyama to become a mole for him, endangering the students' lives numerous times. He and his classmates become newly determined to defeat All For One in the coming battle to decide the fate of the world. He and Aoyama ended up drawing him out to a secluded parking lot, whereupon All For One summoned his army of villains in a moment of arrogance and allowed the heroes to spring their counterattack using Warp Gate.
Upon encountering each other again at the floating U.A. High School, All For One jeered at Izuku over the death of Katsuki and the heroes' devastating injuries. Izuku once again lost control of his emotions and was in severe danger of All For One breaking his spine until Mirio calmed him down, informing him that everybody else was still alive and that Katsuki could possibly be revived by Edgeshot. Remembering Banjo's words about controlling his anger, Izuku was able to keep his cool and prepared to fight, asking All For One if Tomura still remained after his forceful merger with him.
All For One, while unaware Izuku was trying to save Tomura, still sensed he had a plan to defeat him that involved the young man, so he bluffed by saying Tomura was gone and that he was the dominant personality. He was quickly proven to be lying by Mirio, prompting him to lunge at Izuku in another attempt to steal One For All, knowing his control over Tomura weakening meant his window to take back the Quirk was dwindling. However, Izuku had unlocked Gearshift, which allowed him to move far faster than Tomura's body afflicted by Erasure could keep up with. All For One was left stunned and unable to move by Izuku's attacks, expressing his petty hatred for him and the heroes before insisting he hadn't lost just yet, vowing that victory or defeat for heroes and villains would be decided very soon.
In the ensuing chaos, All For One found himself overwhelmed with demented fury at Nagant's interference which allowed Tomura to supersede his control of him, suppressing his master beneath all his rage and hatred while he did battle with Izuku in his place. But after Izuku breached Tomura's mental defenses by launching and destroying the individual quirk vestiges comprising One For All into his body, All For One managed to reassert control over his vessel and derided the two young men, saying Tomura was an "imbecile" who'd never made a decision on his own and that Izuku was a worthless boy who'd carelessly destroyed "[his] One For All... [his] Yoichi." Emerging in the real world, weakened and devastated at what seemed to be the permanent loss of his brother, All For One prepared to kill Izuku in retaliation for sacrificing One For All before being stopped by the timely intervention of Aizawa, Hanta Sero, Mashirao Ojiro, and Rikido Sato.
As the heroes left standing filed in through the portals Aizawa had Kurogiri create, All For One explained that "color" left his world once he felt Yoichi's vestige shatter, and he was able to avoid Izuku's transference attacks as a result of not caring about anything anymore. The implication being that, with nothing left to accomplish now that he couldn't reunite with his brother, he had set his sights on killing Izuku in a final act of revenge ahead of his vague goal of ruling the world, determined to end the person he'd come to despise over any other enemy he'd faced in his century long reign of terror. He entered into battle against the wounded heroes once more, using his most powerful attacks indiscriminately as he tried to wipe out the remainder of All Might's and, now Izuku's, legacy for the hurt he believed it had caused him.
In the end though, despite fighting to kill each other, Izuku expressed pity towards All For One. He remarked that he wasn't a monster beyond understanding or even a demon lord; just a lonely man without any friends, something that ultimately proved to be true, as despite his evil nature, it was shown that All For One still loved Yoichi and desperately wanted his brother back no matter what. As a final act of compassion, Izuku adjusted his earlier attack so that the remains of Yoichi's vestige stayed intact, allowing All For One to spend his final moments with his brother. Izuku then eradicated All For One entirely by mashing his and Yoichi's Quirk Factors together with Tomura and the vestiges' aid, who had re-awakened in response to Izuku's remaining ember, ending the man's tyranny for good and saving the world.

== Lady Nagant ==

All For One correctly deduced that dangling her in front of him to make him try to save her was the surest way to stoke his fury, provoking him into coming after him alone. This tactic actually works for a while, and proves how much of an impression Lady Nagant left on him.

Nagant had helped Izuku realize that anybody can fall victim to circumstance, and that poor decisions are not always reflective of an individual.
During the Final War, just as Tomura was about to activate his Decay, Lady Nagant fired a bullet straight towards him, splitting his right arm off. This act has left Izuku completely surprised, knowing that another former enemy of his has now become an ally.

== Dark Might ==
Izuku first met Valdo Gollini when he and the Gollini Family first appeared in Japan to reclaim Anna Scervino after she was taken by the jailbreaker Ginji Kurau. Izuku was shocked at Valdo resembling All Might in his prime, but his Danger Sense was also going crazy, indicating that the new "All Might" was bad news. Shortly after taking Ginji down for betraying the Gollini Family, Valdo confronted Izuku, recognizing him as a hero in training before offering him a chocolate bar. Despite this, Izuku soon realizes that Valdo is nothing like All Might. The mafia boss brushes this off however, deciding that a petulant child can't grasp what he represents before using his Alchemy Quirk to create cameras and recording drones, hacking into Japan's news network to make an announcement.
Valdo announces that since All Might has retired and left "amateurs" to defend the people of Japan, with other countries abandoning them during their time of greatest need, he intends on taking All Might's place as the new Symbol of Peace and "embodiment of justice", declaring that now it's his turn, referencing All Might's final message as a hero. However, Izuku flies up and calls Valdo out for his "justice" saying that he used unnecessary violence and caused massive collateral damage, something that All Might would never do. Izuku soon realizes that while Valdo looks like All Might, he's nothing like him. Valdo simply scoffs, telling Izuku that he'll soon eat his words.
Shortly afterwards, Izuku and his classmates are all taken hostage in Dark Might's new fortress, the Symbol of Paradise. Upon meeting Dark Might again, the latter sees that Izuku can use multiple Quirks and wonders if he's All Might's successor. Izuku tries to attack him, but the imposter holds him in place before using Bologna Smash to send him flying.
Of all the villains he's faced, Dark Might has clearly struck a very bad chord with Izuku (and by extension Katsuki and Shoto) for impersonating All Might and using his beloved hero's image as motivation for his crimes. Upon partnering with Giulio Gandini to rescue Anna, Izuku soon learns that Dark Might is truly the head of the Gollini Family, the most dangerous criminal organization in Europe who wishes to destroy U.A. High School so he can rule over all of Japan. Izuku doesn't heed to Dark Might's pontificating, clearly not seeing him as the hero that he pretends to be and faces him to stop his evil plans and to save Anna. After a ferocious battle, Izuku manages to defeat an empowered Dark Might by obliterating his monster form and destroying his whole fortress.

== Predecessors ==
The past users of One For All and Izuku's predecessors. Izuku senses their presence for the first time during the Sports Festival, when they briefly intervene to save him from Hitoshi's brainwashing. Months later, Izuku has a dream where he meets the predecessors properly and is shocked to learn that they are all conscious. According to All Might, Izuku is the first user of One For All to be able to communicate with the predecessors. The predecessors have difficulty communicating with Izuku, but they support him in his journey. It's to note after the fight with Nine, all the predecessors chose to stay with Izuku after he transferred the power to Katsuki, recognizing his willingness to protect others, even if it meant losing the power. Almost all the vestiges of the previous predecessors are seen welcoming him as one of them. Despite the fact that All Might no longer has the power, he can still sense his vestige within the vestige world. The predecessors are shown to care for Izuku, as the inheritors of One For All tell Deku not to push himself because of his injuries. They all share the one common enemy: All For One, the arch-nemesis of One For All.

== History ==
As a child, Izuku Midoriya greatly admired Heroes and dreamed of being a hero like his idol, All Might, once he got his Quirk. However, by the time children his age manifested their powers, Izuku still showed no signs of developing any special abilities. A doctor's visit revealed the devastating truth: Izuku has two joints in his pinky toe, a telltale sign that he was born Quirkless.
Izuku had trouble accepting this at first and hoped for a miracle while he was still young. He tried desperately to use telekinesis or breathe fire (his mother and father's Quirks respectively), but to no avail. Izuku would eventually have to face reality: he was one of the very few people who would never develop a Quirk.
Later on, Izuku kept watching videos of All Might that night on his computer and tearfully asked his mother if he could still become a hero. She hugged him and apologized, words that Izuku would remember as not what he wanted to hear because they made him feel inadequate to those with Quirks.
Even so, Izuku still kept aiming to be a hero and to get into U.A. High School. This ambition led him to be constantly bullied and ridiculed by his classmates, especially his childhood playmate, Katsuki Bakugo, who believed it impossible for someone without a Quirk to ever become a hero. Despite this, Izuku still considered the two of them to be friends at the time, always following him around.
However, things escalated when Izuku tried to help Katsuki after he fell off a small bridge into a stream. Izuku offered a helping hand, but Katsuki's pride caused him to take this as an insult. He believed that Izuku made him appear as if he needed aid from someone who is beneath him. This would strain the relationship between the two for years to come, and Katsuki would relentlessly bully him from that day forward. Despite coming to fear Katuski and considering him a jerk, Izuku continued to admire him for his strength and skill.
During his second year of middle school, Izuku was reading a hero magazine at the Tatooin Shopping District when he ran into Katsuki and his two friends. Izuku was then bullied by the three boys, with Katsuki saying that a useless Quirkless boy like him could do nothing but dream. At that moment, a villain suddenly appeared, using his Quirk to rob wallets and purses. Katsuki decided to chase down the villain, hoping to get into U.A. High School on recommendation. As Izuku continued to admire Katsuki for continuing to fearlessly chase after the villain, even after a powerful storm came in, he realized that the villain's Quirk was likely sand-based rather than smoke-based and began searching the alleyways for him. Izuku eventually found the villain after they had already been found by Katsuki and Airjet, the latter of which recommended that Izuku stay back while he praises Katsuki. Before leaving, Izuku thought that Katsuki, being able to deduce the true nature of the villain and arrive before him, makes him incredible despite his unpleasant personality.
